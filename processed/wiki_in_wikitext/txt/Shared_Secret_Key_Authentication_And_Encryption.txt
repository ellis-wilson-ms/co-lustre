=== Introduction ===

Shared Key, added in Lustre 2.9 ([https://jira.whamcloud.com/browse/LU-3289 LU-3289]), offers:

* Isolation (all SSK flavors)
** Prevents clients from mounting without the shared key
** Binds a key to a nodemap, so it is accepted only from that nodemap's NID ranges (with [[UID/GID_Mapping|UID/GID Mapping]])

* Message Integrity (<code>ska</code>, <code>ski</code>, <code>skpi</code>)
** Prevents man-in-the-middle attacks
** Ensures RPCs cannot be altered without detection. <code>ska</code> covers only non-bulk RPCs; <code>ski</code> and <code>skpi</code> also cover bulk data.

* Privacy (<code>skpi</code> only)
** Prevents eavesdropping
** Encrypts RPCs and bulk data

* Ability to choose security flavors between OSS, MDS, MGS, and client nodes, as site policy dictates
