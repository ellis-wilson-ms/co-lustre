--- Comment (zam, Sun, 9 Aug 2026 15:23:31 +0000) ---
I have several reports about this kind of mdd_dir_page_build() error followed by memory corruptions in different areas of Lustre. Eventually I got an ftrace log and even a crash dump for the moment of returning -22 from mdd_dir_page_build().

In all data I have – the mdd_dir_page_build() was called while processing MDS_READPAGE RPC from an ordinary client:

 #9 [ffffb8c0a525fcf0] mdd_readpage at ffffffffc1737d32 [mdd]
#10 [ffffb8c0a525fd20] mdt_readpage at ffffffffc179bdd1 [mdt]
#11 [ffffb8c0a525fd58] tgt_request_handle at ffffffffc14c9273 [ptlrpc]
#12 [ffffb8c0a525fdd8] ptlrpc_server_handle_request at ffffffffc14789cf [ptlrpc]
#13 [ffffb8c0a525fe38] ptlrpc_main at ffffffffc147a63f [ptlrpc]
#14 [ffffb8c0a525ff10] kthread at ffffffffaf9043a6
#15 [ffffb8c0a525ff50] ret_from_fork at ffffffffb020023f 

The unusual thing is that the FID was the master object's FID of a striped directory and I saw (in the vmcore) that the LOD layer did read of both of the stripes, local one and remote, returning -22 at the moment of iterating over the remote stripe:

  lti_it = {
    lit_obj = 0xffff98ee518b43c0,
    lit_stripe_index = 1,
    lit_attr = 7,
    lit_it = 0xffff990d3a057480
  },

I think the memory corruption was caused by a bug in osp_md_index_it_next(), recently discovered and fixed in LU-19798. 

the osp_it state fully matches the description of the bug:

crash> osp_it 0xffff990d3a057480 -x
struct osp_it {

crash> 

as you can see, both ooi_cur_page and ooi_cur_idxpage point to the new page while ooi_net is within the old page.

Why a client may read a striped dir using an old non-dne request, not clear for me.
