--- Comment (emoly.liu, Fri, 17 Jul 2026 03:26:00 +0000) ---
This issue happened because a race condition occurred: object was acceptable to the request path, but LOHA_EXISTS was clear when  mdt_getxattr()  ran. With TCU + striped parent in LU-20305, this is especially likely for virtual STCU FIDs.

BTW, I can reproduce this issue on arm rocky9.6 kernel 100%, but can't on rocky8.10.
