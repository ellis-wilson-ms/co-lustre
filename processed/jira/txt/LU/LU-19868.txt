This issue appears to be related to patch LU-19763.

LU-12945 disabled the zero-copy feature directly on VMs, based on the rationale that "when running on a hypervisor platform, zero copy buffers may still be referenced when write queue size is zero."

LU-19763 re-enabled zero-copy on VMs by introducing the `sendpage_ok()` check, which brings a significant performance improvement. While `sendpage_ok()` provides some protection against `PageSlab`, it doesn't seem to fundamentally resolve the issue raised in LU-12945.

Another reason for the `sanity test_807a` timeout is that the `master + b2_15` version combination has NI Recovery issues after send failures.
