--- Description ---
%Lc and %LS print the internal default sentinel (1152921504606846978 or 0x1000000000000002, aka LLAPI_LAYOUT_DEFAULT) as a literal stripe count or stripe size, respectively.

%Lo will treat the sentinel as a number of OSTs to iterate over and as a result fill the output buffer with "?" placeholders until truncation.

%Li has a related error when the selected layout has no OST object entries.
