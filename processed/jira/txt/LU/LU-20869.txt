Notes

	3 lustre-initialization (Deferred): an Autotest setup step, not in lustre-release; it needs a different fix.

	6 lnet-selftest test_smoke (Deferred): the batch needs about 500 s but SLOW=no caps the run at 300 s, so an early exit saves nothing; a real saving means less coverage. It also fails often (LU-16880, LU-16876).

	7 sanity-dom test_cleanup (Deferred): a cleanup step, not a test body; not yet reviewed.

	10 sanity test_900 (No change): the full unmount and remount is the test; the 20 s fail_loc pause is a small part of the time.

	13 sanity-sec test_31 (Deferred): reconfigures the LNet NIDs on every node and restarts the servers, so most of the time is mount cycles that the test needs; no obvious loop to remove. Revisit if a measurement shows otherwise.

	14 sanity test_123h (No change): 10001 file creates with touch and the statahead scan; the time is file-system speed, not a loop that grows, and createmany cannot make its zero-padded names. One baseline run failed with an I/O error (not investigated).

	16, 20 sanity test_133g and test_133f (Patch): both ended with a full cleanup and setup remount (60 to 150 s) after writing junk to proc files, which resets tunables the tests damage; the remount cannot simply be dropped, so 133g was folded into 133f and one remount follows both. 133g failed in 9 of about 78,000 suite runs in 90 days; its Maloo history ends. A standalone ONLY=133f now also runs the server part.

	17 sanity test_154g (Patch): the cost was llapi_fid_test test20, which builds 2000+ nested directories and calls fid2path on every level.

	24 sanity test_123f (No change): examined with the 123 series; nothing worth changing.

	Rows marked "Not reviewed" have not been looked at yet.
