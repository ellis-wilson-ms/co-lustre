--- Description ---
Several ioctl() for directly interacting with OBD devices are unused by Lustre userspace utilities. Generally, userspace should not interact directly with OBD devices. If a userspace program want information about an OBD device, it can look at the global device list. If it wants to mutate an OBD device, it should use well-defined APIs.
