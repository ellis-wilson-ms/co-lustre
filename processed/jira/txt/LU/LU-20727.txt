Patch Watcher's reproduce-and-fix run, on Patrick Farrell's behalf, reproduced this on unpatched master 4a7bddaf15 with sanity-quota test 58, Rocky 9 (5.14-rhel9.7), ZFS 2.4.0, one MDT and four OSTs. The first run hit tgt_grant_incoming() with grant -1703936.

After an idle OSC reconnects, concurrent DIO writes can consume grant allocated for another write's reply. If that write fails with EDQUOT, cleanup subtracts the full reply grant even though the export no longer holds it, making the grant counters negative. The fix caps that subtraction to the remaining grant and restores server units for clients without GRANT_PARAM: https://review.whamcloud.com/c/69572

With the fix, test 58 passed 15/15 runs (60 to 320 seconds); the grant mismatch recurred without an LBUG. The change remains under review. LU-19087 has a matching crash signature, but the retained results do not record confirmation of duplication or a shared cause with LU-11288 or LU-20523; those relationships remain unconfirmed.
