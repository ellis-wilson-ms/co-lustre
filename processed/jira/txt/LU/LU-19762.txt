--- Comment (hornc, Mon, 10 Aug 2026 15:32:55 +0000) ---
Root cause is a test-side race, not an LNet defect. Tracked in LU-19954: https://jira.whamcloud.com/browse/LU-19954

check_router_ni_status() waits exactly alive_router_check_interval + router_ping_timeout, which is the minimum idle period the router needs before lnet_update_ni_status_locked() will mark the NI down. There is no slack. net_last_alive has a single runtime update site, in lnet_parse(), and that site also sets the NI straight back up. So one unrelated ping arriving during the wait restarts the idle period and the check expires with the NI still up.

test_222, 223, 224 and 225 all open with the same check_router_ni_status call and fail the same way. Test-side fix: https://review.whamcloud.com/67867

Closing as a duplicate of LU-19954.
