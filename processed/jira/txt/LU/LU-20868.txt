--- Description ---
On RHEL >= 7, Fedora >= 18, SUSE >= 13.1 and openEuler, it is not possible to build RPMs without systemd support. Both ./configure --with-systemdsystemunitdir=no and rpmbuild --without systemd are silently overridden.

# rpm -q systemd
systemd-257-13.el10.rocky.0.1.x86_64
# ./autogen.sh && ./configure --with-systemdsystemunitdir=no --with-o2ib=/usr/src/ofa_kernel/x86_64/6.12.0-124.28.1.el10_1.x86_64/ && make rpms
...
# rpm -qpl lustre-2.17.58_117_g7c3ac26-1.el10.x86_64.rpm | grep systemd
/usr/lib/systemd/system/lnet.service
/usr/lib/systemd/system/lsvcgss.service
