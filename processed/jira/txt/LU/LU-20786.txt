--- Description ---
ll_inode_permission() revalidates a fileset mount with IT_GETATTR intent, then it may enqueue lock as LOOKUP|UPDATE|PERM. Then a create or unlink anywhere under the mount root will revoke UPDATE lock and cancels the whole lock. The next permission check then have to go to the MDT to re-enqueue the lock, even though mode, owner, group and ACL that did not change. This may cause excessive enqueue RPC to the MDT, especially when using absolute path in the symbol link.
