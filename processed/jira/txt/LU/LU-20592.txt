--- Description ---
Sometimes reading of  a Lustre striped dir through  an NFS-share goes wrong way using

mdc_read_page() directly instead of using lmv_striped_page_read() and splitting the readdir between the striped on the client side.  

On the server, the read page is handed using lod_striped_it_ops and it hits a bug fixed by LU-19798, see LU-15487 for details.

Looks like the striping info is not available at the moment of doing lmv_read_page() and Lustre client deals with a striped dir as with a plain one.
