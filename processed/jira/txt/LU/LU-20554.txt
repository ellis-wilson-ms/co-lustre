Expected output for lfs quota -g : Sum of reachable OSTs plus MDT, still in parentheses

Cause

lov_quotactl() calculates a valid partial sum, returns `-ENETDOWN` due to an inactive OST, quotactl_ioctl() discards this partial sum. 

The problem affects not only group quotas, but all quota types. Looking at the logic of the functions `lmv_quotactl()` and `quotactl_ioctl()`, it is clear that disabling MDT starting at 1 (not `mdt0`) would also return incorrect information. Furthermore, if some MDTs are disabled, there is no warning about the inaccuracy of the output because `rc=0` and `QIF_SPACE` remain at the end of `lmv_quotactl()`.
