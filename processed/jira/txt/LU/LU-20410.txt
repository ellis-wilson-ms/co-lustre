--- Description ---
Currently, when lustre is built on ZFS debug builds (flag ZFS_DEBUG), the MGS/MDS server panics every time on the first mount and is unable to proceed.

There seems to be something wrong in the correctness of the way Lustre is dirtying the ZFS buffers and how TXGs are included.

The intent is to fix panics in ZFS_DEBUG builds when dnodes are dirtied without being enrolled in the enclosing transaction.

--- Comment (bzzz, Thu, 18 Jun 2026 08:12:09 +0000) ---
this is a know old problem: if we declare all possible writes, then transaction would need huge amount of space (both ram and disk) and the performance suffers a lot.

--- Comment (JIRAUSER18433, Fri, 19 Jun 2026 00:42:39 +0000) ---
I looked at some of these ZFS debug warnings (and I have some draft patches, although not posted yet...). Perhaps we could use dmu_tx_hold_append_by_dnode() / THT_APPEND? It's a fairly new interface, but it's for declaring a write without knowing the exact offsets ahead of time - seems like a fit. Added in 903c3613d490d1321d587982abb5e4d in openZFS.

It would be nice to collab with openZFS to get osd-zfs running cleanly under ZFS_DEBUG.
