--- Description ---
The --restripe option added in LU-16499 is only meaningful for lfs migrate, but it is parsed by the shared lfs_setstripe_internal() and is not restricted to migrate mode. Passing it to lfs setstripe is accepted and leads to a NULL-pointer dereference.

 

Reproduction

[root@rocky-9-bob ~]# touch /mnt/lustre/A
[root@rocky-9-bob ~]# lfs setstripe --restripe /mnt/lustre/A
Segmentation fault (core dumped)
