The LFSCK should be able to validate EC layout constraints:

parity layout ↔ data layout bidirectional link, matching extent and stripe_size,

geometry (dstripe/cstripe non-zero, k+p ≤ 256, self-consistent ec_split_stripes, parity stripe_count = (n0+n1) * cstripe ≤ data stripes),

parity mirror must not mix non-parity components and must start at the mirror boundary. etc
