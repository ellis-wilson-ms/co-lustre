--- Description ---
tunefs.lustre exits with rc 0 without writing anything when --mountfsoptions

is the only change, silently discarding the new mount options (old options

still used on next mount). 

Reproduce:

tunefs.lustre --mountfsoptions= /dev/XXX  (no other option)

then --print shows the opts unchanged. Adding any other option (e.g. --param)

makes it persist.
