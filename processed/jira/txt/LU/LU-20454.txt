--- Description ---
gid2name() in lustre/utils/lfs.c returns unprintable group name.  gid2name() calls getgrgid_r,

whose prototype is        

int getgrgid_r(gid_t gid, struct group *restrict grp,

                      char buf[restrict .buflen], size_t buflen,

                      struct group **restrict result);

In gid2name, it is assumed that group name is stored at &buf[0], that is not guaranteed.

 

uid2name may have the same problem.
