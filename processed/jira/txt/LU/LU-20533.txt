--- Description ---
Don't sleep when a process is exiting. An exiting process can't respond to signals normally and can lock-up the system for an extended period of time. In this case - if we have an alternative to sleeping, we should take it. We don't want to short circuit every sleep - so mimic upstream nfs_current_task_exiting() and annotate known problematic sites.
