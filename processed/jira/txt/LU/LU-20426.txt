--- Description ---
LNet exposed no per-operation latency, only send, receive and drop counts and credit levels in "lnetctl peer show -v". When a cluster performance problem was suspected to be in the network there was no per-peer or per-NI latency to confirm or refute it, and rebuilding it from +net debug timestamps is impractical under concurrent load. LNet should track latency and expose these stats through lnetctl.
