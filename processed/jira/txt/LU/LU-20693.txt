--- Description ---
The lustre-review test sessions has 46 enforced (and 38 optional) sessions per patch, which are consuming all available test cycles and continually leaving 1000+ sessions in the test queue.

Looking through the past two weeks of test sessions to see which ones are redundant and could be removed, it seems like many of the el9.7 sessions are shadowed by el9.8 sessions and appear to have roughly the same failure rates, so it doesn't appear that there is any reason to continue running the old el9.7 sessions, and these ones could be removed:

    review-dne-part-1 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/ldiskfs/ldiskfs
    review-dne-part-3 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/ldiskfs/ldiskfs
    review-dne-part-6 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/ldiskfs/ldiskfs
    review-dne-part-7 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/ldiskfs/ldiskfs
    review-dne-part-8 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/ldiskfs/ldiskfs

The optional el9.8 review-dne-part-4 session looks like it could be enabled to allow the el9.7 sessions to be removed.  The el9.8 review-dne-part-2 and{{review-dne-part-5}} sessions, however, look like they have some persistent errors that do not appear on the el9.7 version.  This might change in the near future due to recent patch landings, so it would be good to keep an eye on these results.

I think the same is true for the enforced el9.7 sessions review-dne-zfs-part-1,2,3,4,6,7, that they could be removed and the existing el9.8 sessions sessions could be run in their place:

    review-dne-zfs-part-1 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/zfs/zfs
    review-dne-zfs-part-2 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/zfs/zfs
    review-dne-zfs-part-3 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/zfs/zfs
    review-dne-zfs-part-4 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/zfs/zfs
    review-dne-zfs-part-6 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/zfs/zfs
    review-dne-zfs-part-7 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/zfs/zfs

The review-dne-ipv6 session on el9.7 also looks very similar to the el9.8 session and could be removed.

    review-dne-ipv6 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/ldiskfs/ldiskfs

The rest of the enforced sessions don't look like they have much overlap and should be kept.

For optional sessions, a couple could be promoted to enforced, as previously described.  Unfortunately, it looks like review-ldiskfs-arm is still having some ongoing issues.

It doesn't provide much value to run optional review-dne-selinux-ssk-part-1,2 sessions for both el9.7 and el9.8, so the el9.7 ones could be removed:

    review-dne-selinux-ssk-part-1 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/ldiskfs/ldiskfs
    review-dne-selinux-ssk-part-2 on el9.7-x86_64/el9.7-x86_64/ldiskfs/ldiskfs

These el9.7 sessions do not seem to fail very often, so they could have been enforced, but there were only two el9.8 sessions in the past 2 weeks, so not much history to go on yet but the el9.8 sessions could be enforced in a week or so if they continue to pass.

The review-ldiskfs-ubuntu session is still having some issues and cannot be enforced yet.
