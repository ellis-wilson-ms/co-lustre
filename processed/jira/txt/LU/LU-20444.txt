--- Description ---
It is beneficial to track when a file was restored successfully to make policy decisions to improve storage utilization.

 

Proposed change is to add `hsm_restore_timestamp` field to hsm_attrs. This will be set by mdt_coordinator after successful hsm_restore and clean up after hsm_remove.
