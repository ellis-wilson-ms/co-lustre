--- Comment (JIRAUSER19288, Thu, 16 Apr 2026 14:04:54 +0000) ---
Very interesting, thanks, Andreas!

I don't think it is this patch ("LU-19648 tests: Add wait_nm_sync in setup_local_client_nodemap") because the changes aren't in the path of test_26. However, it seems that patch "LU-19478 nodemap: clear dyn nodemaps when config is changed" | https://review.whamcloud.com/c/fs/lustre-release/+/62808 is the culprit. The performance numbers correlate with that patch on other branches as well. Looking at the patch now, it seems obvious why. In the case where MGS is colocated with other targets, e.g., the MDT as in Maloo, every nodemap operation through ioctl results in iterating through all nodemaps, which blows up to O(n^2) as opposed to O( n ) before.

I filed LU-20151 to track this. I will look into it for a better solution for checking dynamic nodemaps.
