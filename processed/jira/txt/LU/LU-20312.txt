Hi, I am not sure about the status of this patch. However, I would like to report that I have managed to build and test successfully a lustre client RPM with the attached  spec file. If that helps. 

  magklaraslustrecliebt2170-5.spec  

I would of course have liked to use the upstream Lustre patch for LU-20312 once available/backported. However, time is of essence for us and we need to security patch our systems with the latest and the greatest. 

The latest RHEL 10.2 kernel: 6.12.0-211.18.1.el10_2.x86_64 #1 SMP PREEMPT_DYNAMIC Thu May 21 10:24:38 EDT 2026 x86_64 GNU/Linux

seems to brake 'timer_container_of(...)'. 

The upstream kernel change is visible as “treewide, timers: Rename from_timer() to timer_container_of()”, committed in 2025 for the 6.16 development cycle. Red Hat appears to have backported this interface change into RHEL 10.2’s 6.12 kernel stream; other out-of-tree modules are reporting the same RHEL 9.8/RHEL 10.2 breakage. 

Just my two cents here. 

PS: We have tested this in a non o2ib ldiskfs install: (--with-o2ib=no --disable-server). However, the patch should work for other configuration combinations.
