--- Description ---
LU-18446 (54a64ea818d9) added a PM QoS constraint to keep the CPUs of an RPC round trip out of deep C-states.  Two problems with it:

1. It covers too few CPUs.  kick_cpu_latency() only lowers the latency of the cpt the primary NID maps to, while the thread consuming the reply runs elsewhere – the caller for a sync RPC, a ptlrpcd for an async one.  A multi-rail peer reached over another interface maps to the wrong cpt as well, since only the primary NID is ever seen.  The wakeups form a chain, so whichever link is left in a deep C-state pays the exit latency anyway and most of the gain is lost.

2. The tunables accept values that cannot work.  ptlrpc.pmqos_latency_max_usec and pmqos_default_duration_usec are stored with no range check: a negative latency is silently ignored by the PM QoS core, a duration that usecs_to_jiffies() rounds to 0 takes and drops the constraint on every RPC, and anything above UINT_MAX is truncated.
