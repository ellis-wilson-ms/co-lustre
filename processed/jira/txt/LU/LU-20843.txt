--- Description ---
The Test-Parameters "ostcount=" and "mdtcount=" options are interpreted inconsistently by autotest: ostcount is the number of OSTs per OSS, while mdtcount is the total number of MDTs across all MDS nodes.  This makes it easy to request the wrong configuration.

Evidence:

	https://review.whamcloud.com/69271 patchset 2 used "Test-Parameters: osscount=2 ostcount=12 testlist=sanity" (and testlist=ost-pools).  The custom sessions ran with 24 OSTs (lustre-OST0000 .. lustre-OST0017), 12 on each OSS:
	
		https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sessions/173662d5-e12b-427e-8bd7-1b5f348ca9cc

		https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sessions/c4fd9851-b86a-4261-af6d-4ab6bffddb91

	
	

	https://review.whamcloud.com/56384 patchset 36 used "Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity mdscount=6 mdtcount=24".  The custom session ran with 24 MDTs total (lustre-MDT0000 .. lustre-MDT0017), not 24 per MDS:
	
		https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sessions/45f3fac5-47b3-4a00-94dd-a5c7eb5ea69a

	
	

So "osscount=2 ostcount=12" gives 24 OSTs, but "mdscount=2 mdtcount=12" gives 12 MDTs.

Both options should use the same meaning.  Since mdtcount= already means the total, and the default review-dne sessions describe the configuration as total counts, it would be least surprising for ostcount= to also be the total number of OSTs, spread across the osscount= OSS nodes.  If that is not practical, please document the per-node meaning of ostcount= wherever the Test-Parameters options are described.
