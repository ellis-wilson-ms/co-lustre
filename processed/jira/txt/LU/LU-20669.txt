--- Description ---
This document presents the incremental Redirect-on-Write data snapshot, reuse the Lustre FLR mirror framework, each snapshot is actual a readonly FLR mirror and provide the point-in-time data recovery for regular files;

Moreover, based on snapshot, it is easy to enhance it to support read-only or writable clone (reflink) for Lustre.
