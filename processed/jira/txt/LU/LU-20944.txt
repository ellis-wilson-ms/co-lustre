--- Description ---
Problem

A router that marked a peer NI down drops every message routed to that

peer but never degrades its health. The peer NI stays at

LNET_MAX_HEALTH_VALUE, so it is never queued for recovery, no recovery

ping is sent, and the router black-holes the peer until LNet restarts.

Seen in LUS-13527: 5 of 13 routers black-holed an MDS peer for ~30

minutes, evicting clients. Fingerprint on an affected router:

state: down

health value: 1000

Root cause

On a router the forward runs inside lnet_finalize(): it health checks

the receive leg, then lnet_complete_msg_locked() calls lnet_send() to

forward (lib-msg.c:411-433). The forward fails in

lnet_post_send_locked(), which skips lnet_finalize() because do_send is

false, and the error returns to "if (rc != 0) goto again;" and is

discarded. The health check already ran, for the receive.

Two effects hide it: msg_ev.status is still 0 while msg_health_status is

not, which lnet_is_health_check() rejects as inconsistent; and

lnet_send() overwrites the precise status with REMOTE_ERROR.

The same gap applies to the ACK send, not just routed forwards.
