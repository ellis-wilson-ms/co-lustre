LU-19684 tests: skip conf-sanity/81 for old clients

Some formerly-valid OST index numbers were reserved for overstriping,
and are now considered invalid.  The OST numbers between 65503-65532
are randomly generated in conf-sanity test_81 and are expected to be
valid, so this subtest needs to be skipped for older clients.

Test-Parameters: trivial
Test-Parameters: testlist=conf-sanity env=ONLY=81,HONOR_EXCEPT=y clientversion=2.15
