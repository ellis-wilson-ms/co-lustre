Remove redundant check for 'result' under ll_page_mkwrite0()

required as when checking for !PageDirty(vmpage) 'result' is just
marked -EAGAIN without checking for result == 0.
The same pattern is followed under vmpage->mapping == NULL check
removing the redundant result check
