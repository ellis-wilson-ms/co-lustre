LU-20099 nrs: use nrs_ratecfg data struct to manage rate conf

Move the rate configuration of a rule into the new struct nrs_ratecfg,
so that TBF and mClock share one form. The structure holds the rate
mode, the RPC rate and the byte rate. The structure also holds the
cost of one RPC and the cost of one page.

nrs_ratecfg_adjust() fills the derived fields. The function is the one
place that sets the default rate of a rule. A byte rate below 1 MiB/s
gives 0 RPC/s, so the function sets the RPC rate to at least 1. The
division that follows is then safe.
