LU-20245 lnetctl: Fix buffer overflow in import --old-api

The fgets() loop that slurps the import YAML into a heap buffer
advanced 'buf' before recomputing strlen(), so the subsequent
'len -= strlen(buf)' always subtracted zero. 'len' therefore stayed
equal to the original allocation while 'buf' walked forward,
causing fgets() to be called with a length larger than the
remaining writable space. Newer glibc fortify (using
__builtin_dynamic_object_size on the malloc'd region) detects the
mismatch and aborts with "*** buffer overflow detected ***",
core-dumping lnetctl on the first multi-line import. This is hit
immediately by sanity-lnet test_170.

Capture strlen(buf) into a local before advancing 'buf' so 'len'
shrinks correctly, and guard the loop against len reaching zero.
