LU-6142 obdclass: fix buffer overrun in lustre_get_jobid

Replace strnstr() with strstr() when checking obd_jobid_name
for "%j". As it scans past the end of the 32-byte global
(the kernel's strnstr() walks the full length and does not stop
at the NUL).
