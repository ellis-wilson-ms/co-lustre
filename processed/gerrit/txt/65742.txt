LU-0000 dbg: trace llz4 module init failure on Ubuntu 6.8

crypto_register_alg(&alg_lz4) returned rc=0
crypto_register_scomp(&scomp) returned rc=-22 (-EINVAL)

so it looks like it looks like -22 is returned from
`lz4_mod_init()->crypto_register_scomp()` because `struct scomp_alg`
changed and cra_name is initialized at the wrong offset.
