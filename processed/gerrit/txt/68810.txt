LU-20720 osd: attributes and a private otable iterator

The otable iterator returned a FID and nothing else, so every
consumer that wanted an object's attributes paid an iget for them.

DORA_ATTR asks the iterator to fill the attributes it already has
in hand while it holds the object, and DOIF_ATTR says the caller
wants them.  On ldiskfs that is the inode the iterator has already
read; on ZFS the dnode's bonus buffer.  Neither backend does an
extra read to answer it.

DOIF_PARALLEL gives the caller a private iterator rather than the
one the OI scrub shares, so a scan neither drives a running scrub
nor is driven by it, and several may run at once.
osd_otable_it_next_private() is the private-iterator next.

Both backends carry the same two flags, so a consumer asks the
same question of either.
