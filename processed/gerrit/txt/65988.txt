--- Commit Message ---
LU-20273 misc: fix orr_offset src/dst in endian helpers

Both orr_cpu_to_le() and orr_le_to_cpu() read orr_dst->orr_offset
on the RHS instead of orr_src->orr_offset.  When dst != src this
silently skips the copy, leaving orr_offset unconverted.
