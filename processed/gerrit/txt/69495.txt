LU-20315 ptlrpc: mark module init functions __init

The helpers that ptlrpc_init() calls once at module load, and the
helpers they call in turn, are only reachable during initialisation:
request cache and XID setup, connection table, LNet portals, lproc
and sysfs registration, NRS, the pinger, the HR threads, sptlrpc and
its null, plain and gss flavours, nodemap, early reply size and the
wire constant assertions.

Mark them __init so the kernel places them in the init section and
frees them once ptlrpc_init() returns, instead of keeping them
resident for the life of the module.

Test-Parameters: trivial
