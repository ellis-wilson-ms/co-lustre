--- Commit Message ---
LU-17000 osd: handle OOM in the local sequence scan

GOTO(label, rc) does not assign its second argument, so the bare
GOTO(out, -ENOMEM) in osd_scan_O_seq() left rc at 0 and the scan
carried on.  os_ls_count was left past os_ls_size, so every later
walk of os_ls_fids[] ran off the end of the array.  Set rc and roll
the count back.  On ldiskfs osd_scan_dir() still discards the
callback rc, so there the rollback is what fixes it.
