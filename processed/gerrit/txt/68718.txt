LU-17000 lnet: fix double free when adding a duplicate NI

lnet_startup_lndnet() returns -EEXIST from two places.  Its own
ni_interface check runs before the NI is started and leaves
lnet_dyn_add_ni() to free the NI and the net; lnet_startup_lndni()
returns it after lnd_startup() succeeded, and failed1/failed0 then
shut the NI down and free both.  lnet_dyn_add_ni() freed the same
two objects a second time.  Only the second path has no interface
name, so "lnetctl net add --nid <existing nid>" with no --if reaches
it and faults the node in lnet_ni_free().

Give lnet_startup_lndnet() sole ownership of the net and its NIs,
superseding 239c03e715: leave the duplicate NI on net_ni_added,
where lnet_net_free() still frees it before the net, and drop the
free from lnet_dyn_add_ni().
