LU-20639 ldlm: unlink pingless exports from the timed tree

exp_timed is only ever set, never cleared, so a client that first
connected while suppress_pings was off stays in obd_exports_timed when
it reconnects and is granted OBD_CONNECT_PINGLESS.  It stops pinging
while the server keeps expecting pings, and the ping evictor evicts a
healthy client.

Decide exp_timed on every connect and reconnect, and rename
target_export_timed_link() to target_export_timed_update(), which now
unlinks the export when the answer is no.  Add recovery-small test_26c
for both directions of a runtime suppress_pings change.
