LU-20920 obdclass: keep the lu_site shrinker to its budget

lu_cache_shrink_scan() passes the remaining budget from site to site and
keeps visiting sites after it is used up. Called with nr == 0,
lu_site_purge_objects() still purges one object per hash bucket and
decrements nr to -1, the purge-all value, which it returns as the
remaining budget, so the sites after it are purged completely. As
visited sites are moved to the tail in the same order, the same site
pays the budget on every callback and the one after it is drained.

With two MDTs on one MDS, one debugfs shrinker scan of 128 objects
purged 384. Under an mdtest stat workload one MDT per server was
drained to 40k and 5k cached objects while the other kept 44M and
59M.

Stop the scan once the budget is used up, so the sites not visited
are shrunk first next time, and purge nothing for nr == 0. The
callback again returns the number of objects purged. Fix the comment
on the return value of lu_site_purge_objects().

Add sanity test 435, which fills two MDTs on one MDS with mknod files
and checks with the debugfs shrinker scan that each scan frees at
most its budget and that the scans rotate over both MDTs.
