LU-20409 lnet: Auto-bind NIs to local NUMA node by default

LNet now automatically detects the NUMA node of the physical interface
associated with each NI and binds the NI to the corresponding CPTs.

This ensures NIs are always bound to their physical local NUMA node CPTs,
preventing cross-NUMA performance degradation.

Running lnet_selftest without any configs on two 4-NUMA client nodes
with 200Gbps NIC over tcp.
