LU-0000 lov: fix page ref leak in EC recovery

lov_ec_read_stripe_pages() calls grab_cache_page() taking a reference
to the data page in addition to taking a cl_page reference. But the
cleanup in lov_io_ec_rd_start() only releases the ref for
EC_DPG_UPTODATE pages. The reference on EC_DPG_READ and EC_DPG_ERROR
pages is leaked, so reconstructed pages cannot be freed at unlink and
accumulate orphaned on the file LRU.

This fix releases the ref for EC_DPG_READ/EC_DPG_ERROR pages in both
cleanup paths (rg_cleanup and out). This is similar to how
ll_read_ahead_page() handles it.
