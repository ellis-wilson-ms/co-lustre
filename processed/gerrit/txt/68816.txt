LU-20730 lfu: the Object Stream over OBD_IDX_READ

The transport was already there.  dt_index_walk() drives an index
through init/load/next/key/key_size/rec/store, and the object table
iterator implements every one, having been built as a dt_index for
LFSCK; the container carries variable-size records under II_FL_VARREC
and no keys under II_FL_NOKEY; and rp_attrs carries the iterator
flags from the request to init().  What stood between a client and
the stream was a FID whitelist, an uninitialised feature block, and
the absence of a sender in MDC.  This adds those, and the two things
the walk turned out to need that the design did not see.

The record is built where the OSD is.  dt_otable_lfu_rec() in
obdclass builds struct lfu_rec and its tail from rec(DORA_ATTR) and
rec(DORA_XATTR), which any otable iterator serves; the ldiskfs
iterator answers rec(DORA_LFU) and rec_size() with it, and lfu.ko
calls it for its ring.  One builder, so what leaves through the ring
and what leaves through an RPC are the same bytes, and lfu.ko no
longer counts xattr tiers the OSD already counts.  The ldiskfs
iterator builds the record once per object and keeps it, because the
page builder asks its size before it asks for its bytes.

LL_IOC_LFU_SCAN on a Lustre directory asks one MDT for a slice of its
stream: llite forwards it to the MD export, lmv to the MDT named in
it, and mdc sends OBD_IDX_READ with a bulk of the caller's pages, the
way osp_it_fetch() reads an index across MDTs.  The ioctl is in the
'f' space with the other LL_IOC_*, since lmv turns any other type
away at the door.  lu_idxpage containers are handed to the caller
whole, records back to back inside each.

The walk lost two records at every page boundary, and the oracle is
what said so: 39 missing of 88051, in adjacent pairs, twenty RPCs.
The page builder notes the hash of the record it had no room for so
the next request resumes there; but the otable iterator's load() is
LFSCK's checkpoint -- start after the hash -- so the resume skipped
it; and dt_index_walk() reads a load() answering 0 as "positioned
before, advance", where the otable's 0 means "on it", so the walk's
next() skipped one more.  LFSCK reads a positive load() as "the table
is over", so the answer could not change under it.  DOIF_INDEX is a
second mode: position at the hash and answer as an index does.
LFSCK never sets it; the sender always does.  88051 = 88051, diff 0.

dt_index_read() takes no dt lock on the object table and reads no
version from it: the object is an iterator, not a file.

On one node, the client the server, 88k files: 20 RPCs, 4891 pages,
19.7 MB of bulk, 4400 records per round trip, 0.110 s against the
ring's 0.102 s.  The number this exists for needs a client that is
not the server, since a round trip that costs nothing measures
nothing: stock lfs find spends 88k of them.
