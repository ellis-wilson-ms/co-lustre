LU-10026 ofd: support for compression counters

the patch adds additional compression related counters:
compr_bytes_read_disk -  bytes were read from disk
compr_chunks_read_disk -  chunks were read from disk
compr_bytes_read_user -  bytes were sent to clients
compr_bytes_read_decompressed -  bytes were sent after decompression
compr_chunks_read_decompressed -  chunks were decompressed
compr_bytes_write_disk -  bytes were sent to disk
compr_chunks_write_disk -  chunks were sent to disk
compr_bytes_write_user -  bytes clients send to server
compr_chunks_rmw -  chunks were involved in read-modify-write
compr_bytes_rmw -  disk bytes were involved in read-modify-write
compr_bytes_rmw_user -  bytes clients sent causing read-modify-write
decompression_time - time in usec spent in decompression

server-side compression counters.
