--- Commit Message ---
LU-20773 tests: remove leak_finder.pl script

leak_finder.pl parsed per-allocation D_MALLOC lines of the form
"alloced 'name': size at ptr" to pair allocations with frees.

It's likely that this script is unused.

Delete the script, its Makefile entry, and conf-sanity test 39 which
only checked that the script could parse both message flavours. Drop
LIBCFS_MEM_MSG() and the "name" argument on LIBCFS_ALLOC_POST(),
LIBCFS_FREE_PRE(), OBD_ALLOC_POST() and OBD_FREE_PRE(), since that
argument existed solely for the message. The libcfs_kmem and
obd_memory counters are unchanged; they back the memused proc files
and the test-framework leak check, not the script.

Test-Parameters: trivial
