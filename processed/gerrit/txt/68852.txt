LU-17296 tests: compare one-tenant mClock against FIFO

"mclock jobid+nid weight=1" is a candidate default. One tenant at full
load must not lose to FIFO.

Add sanityn/test_77xi with one fio flow of 16 jobs on one OST. Two
probe passes count the class buckets. No job ID gives 1 bucket. A job
ID for each process gives 16 buckets. Every dispatch must come from
the weight tree.

The test runs FIFO and mClock samples in pairs, in alternate order.
Each pair gives d = ln(mClock IOPS / FIFO IOPS). The test passes when
the 0.9998 Student t lower bound of mean d is above ln(1 - margin).
The test fails when the 0.95 upper bound is below ln(1 - margin). The
test skips after 12 pairs with no verdict.

    NRS_BASELINE_MARGIN    0.20  margin
    NRS_BASELINE_NMAX      12    pair limit
    NRS_BASELINE_RUNTIME   20    seconds for each sample
    NRS_BASELINE_POLICY          policy string
    NRS_FIO_RUNTIME        40    was 120

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanityn env=ONLY="77xi",ONLY_REPEAT=3
