LU-1158 tests: enable nanosecond timestamp test

Now that timestamps carry nanosecond resolution, enable the
pjdfstest utimensat_08 case that exercises sub-second times by
dropping it from the always-except list.  chown_00 stays
excepted under LU-12922.

Also make the sanity timestamp check print the actual and
expected values on failure to ease debugging.
