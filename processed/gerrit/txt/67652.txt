--- Commit Message ---
LU-20545 llite: fix inode ref leak on cached .lustre specials

In ll_iget_for_nfs(), search_inode_for_lustre() takes an inode
reference that must either be transferred to a new dentry via d_add()
or dropped when d_lookup() hits a cached dentry.
There are two such sites under .lustre, including child "fid" and
parent ".lustre" itself. This patch drops the extra ref in both cases.

Fixes: 52a97b4490 ("LU-8585 llite: add special fid handling for fhandle API")
