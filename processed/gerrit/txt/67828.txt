LU-20264 mdc: pass a mnt_idmap to the mdc body packers

Add op_idmap to md_op_data, filled in by ll_prep_md_op_data() from
the mount, and pass it through mdc_pack_body(), mdc_readdir_pack()
and mdc_getpage() so the mdt_body uid/gid fields are mapped through
the mount idmap via lustre_current_fsuid/fsgid(). Like in the
ptlrpc packers, the mbo_uid/mbo_gid slots now carry the fsuid/fsgid
rather than current's real uid/gid.

The mount idmap is still always the identity mapping at this point,
so behavior is unchanged.
