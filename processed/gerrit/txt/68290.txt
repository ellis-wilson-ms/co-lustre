LU-18298 pcc: purge page cache before removing a PCC copy

An encrypted file and its PCC copy share clear text in one page cache
and cipher text in the other, so pcc_inode_put() drops both, reaching
the PCC inode through pcci_path.dentry->d_inode. pcc_ioctl_detach()
removes the copy first, and vfs_unlink() turns that dentry negative,
so the purge finds no PCC inode and the cipher text stays for the next
reader: sanity-pcc test_21j. "lfs pcc detach_fid" reaches this on
master today, as its ioctl never opens the target.

Purge while the copy is still named. Both truncations move to
truncate_inode_pages(), as the _final form sets AS_EXITING for good
and neither inode is evicted here. The attach failure paths and the
refcount gated purge in pcc_inode_put() need S_PCCCOPY cleared as
well, so they want their own patch.
