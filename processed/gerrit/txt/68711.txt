LU-20157 ldlm: do not block lock replay on invalidate_lock

Before replaying locks, ldlm_cancel_unused_locks_for_replay() cancels
the unused locks in the namespace. Cancelling a lock discards its pages,
which takes invalidate_lock exclusively for the whole inode mapping:

  ldlm_lock_replay_thread
   __ldlm_replay_locks
    ldlm_cli_cancel_list_local
     ldlm_lock_cancel
      ldlm_cancel_callback
       osc_ldlm_blocking_ast
        osc_lock_flush
         osc_lock_discard_pages
          __cl_io_init
           vvp_io_init
            down_write            <== waits for invalidate_lock

Meanwhile a read thread holds invalidate_lock shared across its read
RPC, and that RPC is delayed until the import leaves recovery. The
reader waits for replay to finish, replay waits for the reader, and
neither completes.

Skip invalidate_lock for the lock replay thread. That is safe because:

- No page cache fill can be in progress under the lock being cancelled.
  ldlm_prepare_lru_list() selects it with no readers or writers and sets
  CBPENDING, so it gains no new user, and filling a page requires a DLM
  reference on a lock covering it - cl_io_lock() for read and mmap,
  osc_io_read_ahead_prep() for readahead. So the fill that
  invalidate_lock serialises against cannot exist under this lock. And
  also add LBUG in ldlm_lock_addref_internal_nolock() and LASSERT in
  lock_matches() to make sure that.

- A page in the extent may still be in use under another lock. That is
  the LU-16651 case: a reader between ->readpage() unlocking the page
  and its own PageUptodate() check, holding no page lock. That reader's
  lock has l_readers set, so use check_and_discard_cb() even when
  discarding and leave the page to the lock which covers it.

- What keeps a page from outliving its lock does not change. Discards
  still take the page with the blocking cl_page_own() and remove it
  under the page lock, inside the cancelling AST, before
  ldlm_lock_cancel() releases the lock.

Only lock replay is affected. Blocking ASTs, the LRU shrinker, lru_size
and namespace cleanup take invalidate_lock as before.

DoM never set ci_invalidate_page_caches, so it never hold invalidate_lock
during mdc_lock_discard_pages(), i.e. no dead lock issue in this path.

Add recovery-small test_171, which checks that the import recovers while
a read is holding invalidate_lock of an inode with an idle lock on it.
