LU-16554 osp: right-size and check OUT update replies

OSP asks for at least OUT_UPDATE_REPLY_SIZE (4096 bytes) for every
OUT reply, though most need only a few hundred bytes, and
out_handle() packs a reply of the size the client asks for.  OSP
then gets the reply with req_capsule_server_sized_get() and reads
ourp_magic without checking for NULL in osp_xattr_get(),
osp_md_index_lookup(), osp_md_xattr_list() and osp_md_read(), so a
malformed reply fails on the MDT that sent the request.

Ask for a reply sized from the update results, 8-byte aligned as
object_update_result_insert() lays them out, without the 4096-byte
minimum, and have out_index_lookup_pack() and out_read_pack() ask
only for the lu_fid and out_read_reply they get back, since read
data is sent by bulk.  out_handle() has packed the size asked for
since 2.8, so replies also shrink with older servers, and older
clients still ask for 4096 bytes.  Also stop out_read() counting the
bulk data in the result length, which clients only check is at least
the out_read_reply.

Add osp_update_reply_get() to get the reply, check that it is as
large as requested and its magic, and use it for all OUT replies in
OSP, so a bad reply returns -EPROTO as osp_attr_get() and
osp_update_interpret() already did.

Add OBD_FAIL_OUT_REPLY_SHORT to truncate an OUT reply to its header
if the last update type matches fail_val, and sanity.sh test_310d to
check that cross-MDT link, rename and migrate return -EPROTO for a
short OUT_XATTR_GET, OUT_INDEX_LOOKUP and OUT_XATTR_LIST reply.

Cross-MDT mkdir, link, rename, migrate and striped mkdir on 2 MDTs,
about 500 OUT RPCs, expected reply length including headers:

           before  after
  median   4320    304
  mean     4322    730
  max      4344    4344 (linkEA get, caller asks for 4096 bytes)

so the client reply buffer for most OUT RPCs drops from 8KiB to 512B.
