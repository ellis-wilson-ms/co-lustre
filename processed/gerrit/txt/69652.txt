LU-20947 o2iblnd: keep a PUT_DONE with a posted send attached

A scheduler can handle a PUT_ACK before the PUT_REQ send completes.
If connd finalises the conn in that window, kiblnd_queue_tx_locked()
puts the PUT_DONE on ibc_zombie_txs and clears tx_conn. The send
completion then calls kiblnd_tx_complete() with a NULL conn.

A PUT_DONE with a posted send now goes back on ibc_active_txs with
its conn ref. The send completion then releases it.

Fixes: a155c3fca38d ("LU-11756 o2iblnd: kib_conn leak")
