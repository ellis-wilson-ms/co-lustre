LU-20344 lnet: rename lnet_push_update_to_peers()

A later patch makes lnet_push_update_to_peers() also re-ping peers.
The name and the "int force" argument then do not describe it.

Rename it to lnet_resync_peers(). Replace "force" with the enum
lnet_peer_resync_reason:

    LNET_RESYNC_LOCAL_CFG    - the local configuration changed
    LNET_RESYNC_LOCAL_STATUS - a local NI or feature changed status

No functional change.
