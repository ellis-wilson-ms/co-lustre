LU-20935 llite: reject unaligned punch on encrypted files

An encrypted file is stored on the OST as ciphertext, one whole
LUSTRE_ENCRYPTION_UNIT_SIZE (4KB) encryption unit at a time, and the
client encrypts and decrypts a whole unit at once.  ll_fallocate()
already refuses FALLOC_FL_COLLAPSE_RANGE, FALLOC_FL_INSERT_RANGE and
FALLOC_FL_ZERO_RANGE on encrypted files (LU-12275), but it still
forwarded fallocate(FALLOC_FL_PUNCH_HOLE) with an offset or length
that is not aligned to the encryption unit to the OST.

For such a partial-unit punch the OST only clears the requested byte
sub-range of the ciphertext unit and leaves the rest of the unit
intact.  A later read decrypts the whole unit, so a partially zeroed
ciphertext unit decrypts to garbage: both the punched bytes and the
bytes around them are silently corrupted on disk, and the corruption
is visible from another client that has the key.  Page-aligned punches
are unaffected.

Refuse a punch that is not aligned to LUSTRE_ENCRYPTION_UNIT_SIZE on
an encrypted file with -EOPNOTSUPP, instead of corrupting the file.

Add sanity-sec test_49b to check that an unaligned punch is refused
and leaves the file unchanged, and that an aligned punch zeroes
exactly the requested encryption unit and no more.

Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity-sec env=ONLY=49b
