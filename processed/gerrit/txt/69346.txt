LU-12669 lov: keep EC read stripe errors per IO

lovsub_object::lso_status is shared by every IO on the file, but
CIT_EC_RD uses it to decide whether this IO may read a stripe.  An
EC read sets it to LSS_READ_ERR when its sub-lock enqueue fails, and
every EC iter_init resets it to LSS_OK unless the OSC import is
invalid, even for stripes outside its own range.  With an OST that
is down but not deactivated, reader A fails to lock stripe S and
marks it LSS_READ_ERR, reader B starts an EC read of the other raid
set and resets S to LSS_OK, and A then reads S without a lock and
hits the "uncovered page!" LBUG in osc_req_attr_set().

Track the failure in this IO's own lov_io_sub as well:
lov_ec_read_stripe_pages() reads a stripe only if this IO added it
at iter_init and did not skip or fail its sub-lock enqueue or BRW
read.  lso_status remains a hint shared between readers.

Add sanity-ec test_49e, which LBUGs within a few passes without
this fix.
