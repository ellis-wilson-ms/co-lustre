LU-18511 tests: do not destroy krb5 creds in test_90

sanity-krb5 test_90 fails regularly with 'dbench exit with error'.
The client console shows, right after a context flush:

  gss_kt_update()) lustre-MDT0001_UUID at 10.240.43.248@tcp:
  negotiation: rpc err 0, gss err 70000
  ll_close_inode_openhandle()) mdc close failed: rc = -1

Do not flush existing GSS contexts (lfs flushctx) AND destroy Kerberos
credentials (-k) while dbench is still doing it, as Lustre will not be
able to recreate new GSS credentials if Kerberos creds are gone.

Also improve dbench logging to ease debugging.
