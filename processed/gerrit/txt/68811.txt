--- Commit Message ---
LU-20720 osd-ldiskfs: read the inode table ahead of the scan

A private DOIF_PARALLEL iterator walks the inode table in order,
so the block it wants next is the block after the one it has.

Issue the reads ahead of the walk rather than waiting for each
block in turn.  ldiskfs already reads 32 blocks ahead for its own
walk; this makes the scan's readahead explicit and its own, so a
private iterator does not depend on the shared path's window.
