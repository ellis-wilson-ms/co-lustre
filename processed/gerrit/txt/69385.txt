--- Commit Message ---
LU-20869 tests: speed up sanity-sec test_27c

test_27c added and later removed 255 alternate filesets
with one do_facet round trip per fileset, 510 in all, so
it is dominated by process and ssh startup. It averages
7.6 minutes in CI.

Run each of the two loops once on the MGS instead. The
same filesets are added and deleted in the same order,
the overflow check is unchanged, and a failure still
reports the fileset that could not be added or deleted.

On a local single-node VM the test went from 112 and 110
seconds to 45 and 40 seconds.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity-sec env=ONLY=27c
