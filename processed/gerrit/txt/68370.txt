LU-10026 csdc: enable zstd/zstdfast as compr type in layout

The Zstandard compression algorithm shows good promise for both higher
compression than gzip, while also being faster.
Available compression levels for zstd are 1 through 22. In addition,
faster levels supported by zstdfast are 1-10, 20-100 (in increments of
10).
