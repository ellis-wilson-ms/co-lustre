LU-17262 tests: fix replay-single test_135 "Unexpected sync success"

test_135 dirties 20 files, fails ost1 over into a lock replay phase
that is made to fail (OBD_FAIL_LDLM_LOCK_REPLAY), and expects a
concurrent sync to block until recovery restarts and completes.

The check is racy.  It relies on the just-written pages still being
dirty when sync runs: sync then writes them back through ost1, the
import is not FULL during recovery so the write is delayed, and the
sync blocks as intended.  But those pages may be written back to ost1
before it is stopped.  They are not lost -- they are held on the client
as unstable data and replayed -- but they are no longer dirty, and a
plain sync(2) only writes back dirty pages (CL_FSYNC_LOCAL; llite has
no ->sync_fs, so it does not force a commit of unstable data).  With
nothing dirty, the backgrounded sync returns before the import reaches
REPLAY_LOCKS and the test aborts with "Unexpected sync success".

The window widened once client writeback became more eager, which is
why the failure rate rose, but the assumption that the pages are still
dirty when sync runs was never guaranteed.  Before fab71963c2 the check
used "local PID=$?" instead of "$!", so kill -0 tested the wrong pid
and the assertion never fired; making the check real exposed the race.

Hold the writes on the client with OBD_FAIL_OSC_DELAY_IO from
when they are issued until the failover, so the data cannot be flushed
to ost1 early and is genuinely dirty when sync runs.  The hold is
cleared before ost1 sets its own fail_loc, as only one fail_loc can be
active per node.
