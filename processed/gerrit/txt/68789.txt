LU-10026 osc: prevent oversized extents for compressed objects

When compressed objects have high compress_rpc_fragment_ratio values,
large extents could contain too many compression chunks to fit in a
single RPC, causing try_to_add_extent_for_io() to crash with
LASSERT(data->erd_page_count > 0) when the oversized extent was the
first one being considered.

This client-side bug also caused server-side buffer overflows where
the client would send thousands of compression chunks in a single
RPC, exceeding the server's PTLRPC_MAX_BRW_PAGES limit and
triggering 'too large request' errors.

Instead of splitting extents after they are created, this fix
prevents the problem at the source by limiting extent size during
extent creation in osc_extent_find(). For compressed objects, the
maximum extent size is now calculated based on compression chunk
limits to ensure extents never exceed the RPC fragment ratio.

This proactive approach prevents both client crashes and server
buffer overflows while maintaining optimal performance by avoiding
the overhead of extent splitting operations.
