LU-20294 lnet: preserve locked-primary peer merge conflicts

A locked-primary peer conflict occurs when discovery attempts to merge
a NID that already belongs to another locked multi-rail peer.

Such conflicts were marked as BAD_CONFIG but still reported as
successful updates, allowing discovery processing to continue as if
the merge had succeeded.

Return -EALREADY for locked-primary conflicts and preserve that error
through discovery merge processing.

This prevents failed peer merges from being silently accepted and
ensures that persistent locked-primary conflicts are not treated as
successful peer updates or repeatedly retried as transient discovery
failures.
