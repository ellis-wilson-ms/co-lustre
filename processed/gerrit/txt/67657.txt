LU-17000 osc: Add LASSERT() after call to ldlm_handle2lock()

Coverity complains of "Dereference null return value".
'matched' actually cannot be NULL here. So a check for
'!matched' is incorrect here. This patch adds LASSERT()
similar to what is done on other places after call to
ldlm_lock_match_with_skip() -> ldlm_handle2lock()
