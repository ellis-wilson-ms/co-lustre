LU-20861 llite: stop logging s_count in ll_umount_begin()

Linux commit v7.2-rc1-7-g3ec9800c2d33 ("super: convert s_count to
refcount_t s_passive") renamed superblock field s_count to s_passive
and changed its type to refcount_t, breaking the s_count reference in
ll_umount_begin().

Remove the passive reference count from the D_VFSTRACE message to
avoid compatibility code for diagnostic output. Continue logging
s_active.
