--- Commit Message ---
LU-20548 obd: show 'network' server mount option

The 'network=' server mount option was added by LU-18637, but
server_show_options() does not include it in the reported mount options.
As a result, /proc/mounts and userspace commands such as mount and
findmnt omit the option even though it is applied.

Report lmd_nidnet from server_show_options(). Extend sanity-sec test_31
to verify /proc/mounts on each MDT and OST.
