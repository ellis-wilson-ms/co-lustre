LU-20253 idl: reserve OBD_CONNECT2_RENAMEAT2 flag

Reserve OBD_CONNECT2_RENAMEAT2 at 0x800000000000ULL for renameat2()
flags such as RENAME_WHITEOUT.  Only the flag, obd_connect_names[]
and the wire checks are added here so the value can land before the
feature patch.
