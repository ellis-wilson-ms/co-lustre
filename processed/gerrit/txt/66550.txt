--- Commit Message ---
LU-1158 compat: add nanosecond timestamp helpers

Introduce helpers used by the upcoming epoch-seconds to
epoch-nanoseconds timestamp conversion, with no call-site
changes yet so this patch is a pure addition:

- inode_get_[amc]time_ns() return inode timestamps as s64
  nanoseconds since the epoch.
- A KTIME_SEC_MIN / lu_timespec64_to_ns() shim for kernels
  older than 5.15, where timespec64_to_ns() mishandles
  negative tv_sec.  This is conditional on the kernel lacking
  KTIME_SEC_MIN so it disappears on newer kernels.
