LU-20907 ptlrpc: accept final ping that races recovery end

A client whose final recovery ping times out reconnects. If the
target is still recovering, the client goes back to REPLAY_WAIT
and sends a new final ping. When recovery completes between the
reconnect and the arrival of that ping, ptlrpc_check_req() sees
REQ_REPLAY_DONE on a non-recovering target and fails the export,
evicting a client that recovered successfully.

The target code already handles a final ping that arrives after
recovery as an ordinary ping. Let it through when the export has
already queued its final ping in this recovery.

Add replay-single test_93c. It holds the first final ping on the
OST until it times out, as test_93a does, then delays the client's
second final ping with the new OBD_FAIL_PTLRPC_DELAY_FINAL_PING
until recovery has finished.
