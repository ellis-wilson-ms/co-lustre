LU-19157 nodemap: do not apply ban list on the MGS

The ban list is checked in tgt_request_handle(), which lets connect,
disconnect, ping and close through, but only for MDS and OST. On the
MGS, the MGS_CONNECT of a banned client gets -EPERM, after the export
has been created. The client treats this as a transient connect
failure, so the MGC import never reaches FULL and keeps reconnecting.

Fix this behavior by excepting the ban for requests needed by the MGC
to fetch the configuration. A banned client now gets its
configuration, connects to the MDT, and its first MDT request fails
with -EPERM, so the mount fails immediately with "Operation not
permitted".
Adding only MGS_CONNECT/MGS_DISCONNECT to the exempt list is not
enough. The MGS lock request would then fail with -EPERM, but the MGC
still treats a failed lock request as "MGS unreachable" and keeps on
retrying.

Improve sanity-sec test_81a to check that a mount from a banned
client fails within 30 seconds and with EPERM.
