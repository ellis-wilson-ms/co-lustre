LU-20690 utils: per-stripe parity I/O helpers

Prepare userspace for the RAID10-like EC parity layout, where the
p parity objects of a raidset share one file offset and must be
addressed by (pos, stripe) through LL_IO_SCOPE_STRIPE.

Add llapi_mirror_read_stripe() and llapi_mirror_write_stripe().
They share one path with llapi_mirror_read()/llapi_mirror_write()
via an explicit pin_stripe flag: ordinary mirror I/O keeps using
llapi_mirror_set(), while the _stripe variants pin the mirror and
stripe with LL_IO_SCOPE_STRIPE.

llapi_ec_write_parities() and llapi_ec_verify_parities() take the
parity component instead of stripe size and id, and do parity I/O
through the _stripe helpers. Offsets are unchanged: parity i is
still at ec_pos + i * stripe_size. With the current RAID0|PARITY
encoding lov ignores the designated stripe, so resync and verify
behave as before.

Add llapi_layout_is_parity_mirror() and use it from 'lfs mirror
write' (refuse writing a parity mirror; point to 'lfs mirror
resync') and from mirror_split() instead of a local helper.
