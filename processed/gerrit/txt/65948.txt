LU-20249 lmv: improve quota-aware MDT retry testing

Add MDT-specific EDQUOT injection via fail_val for
independent quota testing. Improve random MDT selection
by adding 1 before get_random_u32_below(count - 1) to
avoid selecting the current MDT and reduce unnecessary
fallback iterations.

Add parameter verification to sanity-quota test_98.
