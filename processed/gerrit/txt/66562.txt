--- Commit Message ---
LU-20344 lnet: add reachable peer discovery mode

Discovery merges each NID from the ping reply of a peer. A client
thus builds peer state for NIDs that it cannot reach, such as the
NIDs of other tenants.

Add mode 2, LNET_DISCOVERY_MODE_REACHABLE ("lnetctl set discovery 2").
The merge then adds only the NIDs on a local net or on a net with a
route. It prunes a known NID that is now unreachable. It keeps the
primary NID and the NIDs of a DLC peer. Discovery uses the first
reachable advertised NID in place of an unreachable advertised
primary. An unlocked peer can then take that NID as its primary.

In this mode, a new or removed local or remote net re-pings the
multi-rail peers. Each mode change that leaves discovery on also
re-pings them. Without kernel support, lnetctl refuses mode 2.
