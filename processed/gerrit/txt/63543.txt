--- Commit Message ---
LU-19824 ldlm: don't allow sysfs reads before ldlm is setup

If you attempt to access per_cpu counters before they are
initialized, you may crash. To avoid this, short-circuit
sysfs read in LDLM until LDLM is properly setup. This is
tracked by a new field in ldlm_state.

Some concerns, see inline comments. The window this closes is real, but a flag re-checked on every read cannot be made airtight against teardown, whereas sequencing the sysfs registration can. sysfs_create_group() currently runs near the top of ldlm_setup() and sysfs_remove_group() near the bottom of ldlm_cleanup(), which is exactly backwards: creating the group as the last step of setup and removing it as the first step of cleanup would make the attributes visible only while the state behind them is valid, would cover every present and future attribute in ldlm_attr_group, and would need no new field and no memory barriers.
