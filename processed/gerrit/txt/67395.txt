LU-20495 lmv: fix use-after-free in qos exclude list

lmv_qos_exclude() walked lmv_qos_exclude_list without a lock while
the qos_exclude_{patterns,prefixes} handlers free entries under
lmv_lock, so a mkdir racing "lctl set_param qos_exclude_patterns"
dereferenced a freed entry and panicked the client.

Take lmv_lock around the walk, as the seq_show path already does.
