--- Commit Message ---
LU-20786 llite: reduce fileset root permission lock churn

ll_inode_permission() revalidates a fileset mount root with an
IT_GETATTR intent. The MDT normally grants a LOOKUP|UPDATE|PERM lock.

A create or unlink directly under the mount root conflicts with UPDATE
and cancels the whole lock. The next permission check must contact the
MDT to enqueue another lock, even though the mode, owner, group and
access ACL did not change. This can generate excessive enqueue RPCs,
especially when resolving an absolute symlink repeatedly crosses the
fileset mount root.

Use IT_LOOKUP instead. It normally grants LOOKUP|PERM, covering the
attributes consulted by generic_permission() without coupling them to
UPDATE. Match a cached lock on PERM rather than LOOKUP so a DNE
name-only LOOKUP lock cannot satisfy permission validation on the
FID-owning MDT.

An MDT may opportunistically add UPDATE to IT_LOOKUP for an inode with
an old ctime. One namespace update can revoke that lock and cause a
refresh. The update also advances ctime, so the replacement normally
does not include UPDATE. Exercise repeated namespace updates and allow
at most one refresh, while rejecting recurring enqueue RPCs.
