--- Commit Message ---
LU-20515 lnet: prevent peer net list corruption during discovery

When a discovered NID belongs to another peer with a locked
primary NID, lnet_peer_add_nid() rejects the addition but
incorrectly returns success.

During discovery merging, lnet_peer_merge_data() may then move
the advertised primary NID's peer net onto the current peer's
list without updating its ownership or reference count. This
can leave the peer lists inconsistent and trigger an assertion
in lnet_destroy_peer_locked() when the peer is released.

Return -EALREADY when the locked-primary conflict is detected,
preserving the existing peer configuration without triggering
the forced merge behavior associated with -EEXIST.

Only reorder the primary peer net and peer NI when they belong
to the peer being updated. Protect the list operations with
the exclusive LNet network lock to synchronize with readers.
