--- Commit Message ---
LU-20076 tests: fewer remote commands for replay_barrier

replay_barrier() is called about 200 times in replay-single and
replay-dual, and each call runs 10 separate remote commands (9
logged with VERBOSE), including mcreate for each of $MOUNT and
$MOUNT2 on the clients, "notransno" and the "REPLAY BARRIER" mark on
the server, and four dmsetup commands to load a flakey table for
set_dev_readonly().

Create files on $MOUNT and $MOUNT2 in one remote command, send
"notransno" and the mark in one remote command, and suspend, load and
resume the dm device in one remote command in dm_set_dev_table(),
which now also resumes the device if the load fails.  The mark is now
sent just before the device is made read-only instead of just after,
and still returns the "notransno" error.

Remote commands logged with VERBOSE for one replay_barrier() of mds1
with dm-flakey: 9 before, 5 after.  replay-single 0a-8a and 20a-26
pass with the patch.
