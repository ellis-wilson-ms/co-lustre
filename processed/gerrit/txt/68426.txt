LU-19740 tests: except conf-sanity 115 for old clients

86904ef943 ("LU-19740 utils: Restrict {tunefs,mkfs}.lustre to one
device") made mkfs.lustre reject a repeated device, and fixed
conf-sanity test_115, which passed the OST device twice.

The test script comes from the client while mkfs.lustre comes from the
server, so an interop session with an older client still runs the old
test_115 against a new mkfs.lustre and fails.  Except it there.

Test-Parameters: testlist=conf-sanity clientversion=2.16 env=ONLY=115,HONOR_EXCEPT=y
