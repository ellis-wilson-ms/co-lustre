LU-17916 obdclass: fix dt_index_page_adjust() w/ 64K PAGE_SIZE

It has been reported that builds with 64K PAGE_SIZE fail, as "pages"
in dt_index_page_adjust() is undeclared; fix this by using
kmap_local_folio(), instead of kmap_local_page().
