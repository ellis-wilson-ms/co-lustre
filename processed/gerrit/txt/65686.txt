LU-20205 ldlm: no need to check all locks in hpreq_check

Orginally, while canceling LRU locks, we skip BL_AST locks. However,
they may become BL_AST by the time of ldlm_cli_cancel_list_local(),
where we check for BL_AST atomically with setting BL_DONE. Thus next
BLAST RPCs return ENIVAL, whereas ldlm_cli_cancel_list_local() or
ldlm_cli_cancel() issues a separate cancel RPC for previous BLASTs.

It gives us an oppotunity to check for the 1st lock only in
hpreq_check as it is guaranteed a blocked lock gets either EINVAL reply
for a BLAST RPC or a separate cancel RPC.

An interop is needed, because ldlm_cli_cancel_list_local() did not
issue a separate cancel RPC in cases LCF_BL_AST was given but the 1st
lock in the list of locks to be cancelled was not marked as BL_AST,
i.e. a BLAST RPC could came before BL_DONE is set (no EINVAL), but
the lock could be packed to RPC before BLAST handling gets to
ldlm_cli_cancel() - thus ldlm_cli_cancel() dropped it by AST_SENT flag
