--- Commit Message ---
LU-12669 ec: optimize EC recovery crypto for encrypted files

For encrypted files with EC layout, the current implementation wastes
significant CPU by:
1. OSC decrypts pages after reading from OST
2. LOV re-encrypts the same pages for EC recovery
3. LOV decrypts all pages (recovered + available) after EC recovery

This patch optimizes by having OSC save the encrypted page data to
bounce pages provided by LOV, eliminating redundant encrypt/decrypt:
1. OSC decrypts pages AND saves original encrypted data to bounce
   pages
2. LOV reuses the encrypted bounce pages (no encryption needed)
3. LOV only decrypts recovered pages (data pages already plaintext)
