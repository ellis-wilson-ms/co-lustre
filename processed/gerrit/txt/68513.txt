--- Commit Message ---
LU-17000 ptlrpc: compare page counts in nodemap_get_config_req()

The assertion guarding the loop that hands rdpg.rp_folios[] to the bulk
descriptor compares a page count against rdpg.rp_count, which is a byte
count, so it is satisfied by anything short of a 4096-fold overrun and
cannot catch nodemap_index_read() returning more bytes than the folio
array holds.  Compare against rp_npages, the array size, as
mgs_get_ir_logs() does with its own nrpages.
