--- Commit Message ---
LU-20442 ofd: implement per-object/FID-level I/O statistics

Implement high-performance object-level/FID-level I/O statistics
tracking at the Lustre OST Device. It tracks the number of read/write
bytes and operations.

The stats use a 4-way associative hash set with LRU replacement
based on access timestamps.

Add test_500 and test_501 to sanity.sh to verify correct stats collection,
and LRU eviction/replacement behavior.
