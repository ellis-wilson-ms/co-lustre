--- Commit Message ---
LU-20816 osd: keep DIO folios locked on faked reads

The per-thread DIO folios are locked when allocated and stay
locked for as long as the thread caches them; osd_get_folio()
reuses them without locking again.  A read faked with
OBD_FAIL_OST_FAKE_RW marks the folio uptodate and so takes the
cache-hit branch of osd_read_prep(), which unlocks it.  The next
use of that cached folio then trips an assertion: a real write in
osd_write_commit(), or thread teardown in osd_key_fini() when the
OST unmounts, both on ASSERTION( folio_test_locked(folio) ).

Leave DIO folios locked in that branch.  A real read cannot reach
it with one, since osd_get_folio() clears uptodate on each reuse,
and page cache folios never carry private_2.
