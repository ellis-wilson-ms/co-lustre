LU-19403 llapi: Move nodemap activate function

Move the body of jt_nodemap_activate() from obdctl to liblustreapi
as llapi_nodemap_activate(), so it can be reused outside of lctl.
This is a straight move: cmd_name/activate stay strings, since
the MGS accepts several spellings for @activate beyond "1"/"0".
