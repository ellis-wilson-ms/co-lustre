LU-20357 obdclass: clear idle NID statistics on server

Per-NID stats on MDTs and OSTs are kept forever after a client goes
away, so with high client churn they keep growing in memory and in
debugfs. Add nid_stats_idle_time (default 3600s, 0 disables) to free
them once no export has used them for that long.

- When the last export of a NID goes away, its nid_stat is stamped
  with nid_last_idle and moved to the time-ordered obd_nid_stats_idle
  list. A reconnect from that NID takes it back.
- The ping evictor now also wakes every 60s and frees expired entries
  on every MDT and OST, found through a new xarray mark on obd_devs.
- An entry being freed is marked NID_STATS_DISPOSE, and a connect that
  finds it waits for the free to finish. Debugfs is removed before the
  entry leaves the hash, so the new entry does not hit -EEXIST.
- nid_stat is freed with RCU since hash readers may still see it, and
  obd_nid_stats_hash is now destroyed in class_free_dev().
- class_cleanup() waits for in-flight frees via
  obd_nid_stats_inprogress.
- exports.clear now clears active stats and frees all idle ones, and
  exports/<nid>/export reports idle_seconds for an idle NID.
- Rename obd_nid_stats_get/put() to obd_nid_stats_insert/remove().
- The MGS is not covered: its per-NID stats are of no use and should
  be removed instead.
- Add man pages for nid_stats_idle_time and exports.clear, and
  sanity test_921.
