LU-20264 mdc: map reint fsuid/fsgid through op_idmap

Drop op_fsuid/op_fsgid from md_op_data, which were resolved from
current's credentials when the op data was prepared, and instead
map current's fsuid/fsgid through op_idmap at pack time in the
reint record packers (open, setattr, unlink, link, rename, migrate,
resync). Drop the now-unused assignments in llite and lmv.

Also tag the reint requests with rq_idmap so the generic body,
jobinfo and user-desc packers use the same mapping.

The mount idmap is still always the identity mapping at this point,
so behavior is unchanged.
