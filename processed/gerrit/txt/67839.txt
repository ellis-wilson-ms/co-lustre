--- Commit Message ---
LU-20264 nodemap: add trust_client_perms flag

The MDS only sees uc_cap with caps stripped for non-local roots and
cannot reliably verify capabilities held inside a child user namespace
(e.g. CAP_DAC_OVERRIDE for a rootless container), so it would reject
operations the client kernel has already legitimately authorized. Add
a per-nodemap nmf_trust_client_perms flag: when set, the MDS skips its
server-side POSIX re-checks (mode/owner/sticky/cap) and
mdd_permission() returns success in its capability-check fallback,
deferring to the client's decision instead of returning unconditional
-EACCES.

When no nodemap is in force at all, trust_client_perms is enabled by
default so idmapped-mount and userns ops keep working out of the box.
