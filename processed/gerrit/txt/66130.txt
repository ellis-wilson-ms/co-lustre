LU-20299: llite: Reduce addressspace invalidatelock hold time

Holding address space invalidate lock for discard pages case across
entire duration of the io is making it collide with long-running readers
(slow server or network troubles) creating hung ldlm bl threads.
Reduce the hold time to just when we do mappign manipulation - that
still affords us all the necessary protections.

It turns out existing test 833 that was supposed to demonstrate this
condition is not really able to and even doing tests on multiple
mountpoints in sanityn does not help to really demonstrate any fixes
here or in the prior patch as effective. But it was seen in the wild,
mostly on aarch64 with 64k pages.
While the hunt for the reliable reproducer continues, I still wanted to
put this patch out here so people could try it out if they are affected.
