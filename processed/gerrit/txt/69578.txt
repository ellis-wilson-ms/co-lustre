LU-19908 quota: don't refetch a valid slave index on reint

Since LU-19136 a quota slave starts reintegration when a
DQACQ reply carries a newer global index version. With the
global lock already held, that reintegration also transfers
the slave index, while DQACQs resume as soon as the global
index is reintegrated. An acquire granted by the master
after the slave index was read is then overwritten when the
stale record is applied to lqe_granted, so the master keeps
quota space the slave no longer knows it owns. A later limit
for that ID is exhausted early: sanity-quota test_12a and
test_13 fail with EDQUOT when an earlier test, such as
test_7f, has left such a grant behind.

Only transfer the slave index when the slave copy has been
invalidated: at setup, on loss of the global lock and on
force_reint.
