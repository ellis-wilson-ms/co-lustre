--- Commit Message ---
LU-20583 llite: stop statahead upon inode invalidation

Directory migration can race against FNAME statahead pattern thread:

LustreError:...cl_file_inode_init())...failed to initialize cl_object
LustreError:...ll_prep_inode())...new_inode - fatal error: rc = -5

To prevent the race, this patch stops statahead threads when the
client loses the MDS_INODELOCK_{LOOKUP,PERM} lock on the parent.

Add regression test: sanity 123m

Fixes: e10bf68d7c ("LU-14361 statahead: regularized fname statahead pattern")
