--- Commit Message ---
LU-20726 obdclass: validate parameter names in config

Require exact attribute-name matches in class_set_global() and
class_modify_config() to prevent prefix collisions, and reject
missing/empty names with -EINVAL instead of crashing. Add debug
logging for rejected global params while preserving prefix-based
lookups where valid.

Fixes: a74b2b5bce71 ("LU-9431 obd: resolve config log sysfs issues")
