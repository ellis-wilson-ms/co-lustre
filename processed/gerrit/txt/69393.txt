LU-20871 lod: fix NULL deref on unordered do_index_ops read

An MDS servicing MDS_READPAGE can take a NULL pointer dereference in
lod_it_init():

  pc : lod_it_init+0x4c/0xdc [lod]
  Call trace:
   lod_it_init+0x4c/0xdc [lod]
   dt_index_walk+0xb0/0x4e8 [obdclass]
   mdd_readpage+0xc0/0x5bc [mdd]
   mdt_readpage+0x788/0xd00 [mdt]

The NULL is dt_object_child(dt)->do_index_ops. lod_index_try() sets
the layer below's .do_index_ops before its own, but a consumer reads
the two independently: dt_try_as_dir() returns early once the upper
layer's is set, so it neither re-runs ->do_index_try() nor orders the
reads, and a weakly ordered CPU may satisfy the second load before
the first store is visible.

Order the stores with smp_wmb() in lod_index_try(), and the reads
with smp_rmb() in dt_try_as_dir(), where a consumer first observes
the published ops. Consumers of the layer below's index ops and of
the striping fields reach them through dt_try_as_dir(), so the one
barrier there covers the readdir, lookup and insert/delete paths.
