LU-20424 ldiskfs: save dirdata in ext4_htree_store_dirent

ext4's readdir saves direntries to return them hash ordered,
this "saving" procedure didn't take dirdata, so OSD couldn't find FIDs

osd_fid_unpack()) unexpected packed fid size: 0
osd_fid_unpack()) Skipped 14381 previous similar messages

Add a simple test to check for fids in dir entries.

Add new el10.1 ldiskfs series to resolve conflicts with new kernel.
