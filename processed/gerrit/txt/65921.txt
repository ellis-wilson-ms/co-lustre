LU-19548 lfs: mirror extend support for EC

Add three EC modes to 'lfs mirror extend':

--mirror-id N --ec D+P: attach parity to data mirror N.
-N [K] --ec D+P: add K data+parity pairs (default 1).
--ec D+P: attach parity to the file's lone data mirror;
files with 2+ mirrors need --mirror-id or -N.

As with a regular extend, new mirrors are in sync on
success. Populate new data mirrors before merging them.
Merge parity stale with LL_LEASE_ALLOW_STALE (LU-18746),
then compute it through resync. An interrupted parity
computation leaves stale components for a later resync.
Reject known invalid -N --ec geometry before adding data
mirrors; if parity fails before its merge, attempt to remove
the data mirror just added.

Refuse stale or nosync targets and a second parity geometry
on one data mirror (LU-20616). Validate --ec and -f per
mirror so they cannot be combined on the same mirror,
including before a later -N, but can be used on separate
mirrors in either order. Refuse -f with --mirror-id.

Add llapi_layout_comp_add_parity_only() and accept
parity-only layouts. Defer their allocation at create until
resync has the full destination layout; instantiating a
parity without its data component remains an error. In the
round-robin allocator, let a parity's avoidance of unrelated
mirrors relax on later passes. Test 8j checks deferred
allocation with QoS.

Bind the data and parity mirrors in both directions and
record the EC geometry on the data components. Clear that
geometry when parity is removed so later writes and resync
can instantiate the unlinked data tail. Preserve the pair
when splitting both mirrors together. Also reject zero EC
stripe counts to prevent division by zero and an LASSERT.

The stale merge requires OBD_CONNECT2_FLR_EC. Pass the
client module option through MODOPTS_LUSTRE instead of
MODOPTS_LLITE, and enable mdt_enable_flr_ec on the MDT's
node before clients mount. This also covers remote MDTs
whose modules are loaded by mounting the target rather than
by load_modules().

Gate the new sanity-ec subtests on MDS > 2.17.57, as older
servers reject parity-only create buffers. Cover attach
modes, option ordering, and writes and resync after parity
deletion.
