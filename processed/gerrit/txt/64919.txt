LU-12669 lov: slow and dead OST detection for degraded reads

A read of an EC file whose data stripe is on a dead OST waits for
the RPC's adaptive timeout, 7 s in production, before it is
reconstructed from parity; a slow OSS extends that with early
replies, and even a dead import costs the FLR retry rounds first.

Give such reads a soft deadline and route around the OST.  A
request past it fails with -ETIMEDOUT without failing the import or
touching the adaptive timeout.  Each OSC keeps a smoothed OST_READ
latency and deviation per RPC size, as for the TCP retransmission
timer, and a top-level no-delay read of an EC or mirrored file gets
a deadline, counted from its send, of the largest of
osc.*.slow_read_pct (default 300) percent of the smoothed latency,
that latency plus four deviations, and osc.*.slow_read_min_ms
(default 250 ms for a non-rotational OST, 2 s otherwise).  An
abandoned read marks the import slow for four times its deadline;
each further soft timeout before a successful read doubles the
deadline and the mark, up to the adaptive timeout.

LOV treats a deactivated, invalid, disconnected or slow-marked import
as unavailable, but not one reconnecting from idle.  A read of a dead
stripe is reconstructed at once when its raid set's own parity covers
its dead stripes, and of a slow-marked one only when it covers all of
its unavailable stripes; otherwise the read waits for them, e.g.
until an OSS failover completes.  With no in-sync parity the file
reads like a plain mirror.  A read of part of a raid set, whose
reconstruction reads many times its size, gets no soft deadline and is
not reconstructed around a slow stripe when most of the set's other
stripes are on OSTs running at over twice their median latency since
mount and over an eighth of slow_read_min_ms.  A plain mirror's
buffered reads get the same deadline and rotate mirrors on expiry,
unless no other mirror can serve the read.  An EC read skips the lock
and read of a stripe it reconstructs by having no sub-IO for it,
rather than through the lso_status shared with other readers.
osc.*.slow_read_stats shows the estimates and counters.

A 1MiB read of stripe 0 of a 4+2 EC file on an 8-OST VM, with one
30s stall of its OST (OBD_FAIL_OST_BRW_PAUSE_BULK), then 200 1MiB
direct and 200 4KiB buffered random reads while 4 writers load all
OSTs:

  slow_read_pct            0 (off)    300 (default)
  direct read, stall       30053ms    2069ms
  buffered read, stall     30055ms    2048ms
  reads under load: 0 soft timeouts, no extra parity or survivor reads

Also fix lov_io_set_range() rounding the reconstruction range end
past its component, which LBUGged "uncovered page!"; the
ra_cur_pages leak in ll_readahead_handle_work() when
cl_io_rw_init() fails, which stopped readahead for the mount; and
do_file_read_iter() never clearing via_hybrid_switched, which ran
later O_DIRECT AIO reads on the same lu_env synchronously.  Also fix
ll_readahead_handle_work() passing a page index and count to
cl_io_rw_init() as bytes, which routed async readahead of FLR and EC
files by the wrong extent, and the client io_latency_stats arrays
being one size short for a maximum-size RPC.

Add sanity-ec 77a-77j, and change test_29a to check that a read with
more stripes lost than parity waits for their OSTs.
