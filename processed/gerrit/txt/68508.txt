--- Commit Message ---
LU-17000 libcfs: carry the dump pid as a pid_t

libcfs_debug_dumplog_internal() takes a pid through a void * and prints
it with %ld, so smatch reports the argument as cast from a pointer.
Take a pid_t instead, so the value is not laundered through a pointer,
and use uintptr_t on both sides of the round trip through
kthread_run().  The dump file name is unchanged.
