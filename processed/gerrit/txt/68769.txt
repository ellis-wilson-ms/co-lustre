LU-15394 lnet: fix NI leak and LBUG in lnet_startup_lndni

lnet_startup_lndnet() unlinks each NI from net_ni_added before
handing it to lnet_startup_lndni(), so for the duration of the
startup the NI is owned by no list.  An NI refused with -ENOSPC
because lnet_interfaces_max is reached is then never freed, and
the two paths that take a started NI back down - a failed
lnet_ni_set_default_cpts() and a net with no credits - call
lnet_ni_unlink_locked(), which requires a linked and referenced
NI, and trip its assertion.  That crash is LU-15394.

Leave the NI on net_ni_added until it has started, so that
lnet_net_free() reclaims it, and give every post-startup error a
common exit that references the NI before shutting it down.
