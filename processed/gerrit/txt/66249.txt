LU-20311 lmv: fix double-fput in lmv_hsm_ct_register

On a DNE client, if any MDT's HSM coordinator is shut down when a
copytool registers, the per-MDT ioctl returns -ENXIO and the LMV
error path double-fputs the kuc pipe. The next close() of the pipe
fd hits kernel BUG at fs/open.c:1515 and panics.

libcfs_kkuc_group_add() now takes its own ref via get_file() so
group_rem's fput balances it. lmv_hsm_ct_register's cleanup
cascade falls through normally with libcfs_kkuc_group_rem guarded
by if(rc).

Add sanity-hsm test_256 reproducer.

Fixes: 46aee8eba900 ("LU-9416 hsm: add kkuc before sending registration RPCs")
