LU-20288 ksocklnd: fix assert when closing conn

When we have IPv6, ksnc_proto is not ksocknal_protocol_v3x but
ksocknal_protocol_v4x. This fix solves:
ASSERTION( conn->ksnc_proto == &ksocknal_protocol_v3x ) failed in
ksocknal_close_conn_locked.
