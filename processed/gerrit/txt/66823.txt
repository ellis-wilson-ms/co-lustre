--- Commit Message ---
LU-20422 llite: use lockless inode_state_read_once

On v6.18+, inode_state_read() asserts i_lock is held via
lockdep_assert_held(). Lustre's call sites read i_state locklessly
(e.g. the I_NEW check in ll_iget() after iget5_locked()), so the
assertion trips a WARN). Use the lockless inode_state_read_once()
variant instead, matching the original bare i_state reads.
