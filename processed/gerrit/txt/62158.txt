LU-12597 tests: remove comma_list for all_nodes

Remove comma_list for all_nodes, all_mdts_nodes, all_osts_nodes,
all_server_nodes, etc. usage in test-framework.sh, sanity-sec.sh
and obdfilter-survey.sh. These helpers already end in comma_list()
after the earlier patches in this series, so the outer call is a
no-op.

In load_modules() and unload_modules(), rename the local $list to
$nodes to match. Hoist the duplicated empty-list check in
unload_modules() so the lines this rename touches fit in 80
columns.
