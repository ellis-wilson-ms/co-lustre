LU-20776 lfsck: do not scan an OST twice for orphans

Both the OST's LE_PHASE1_DONE notification and the master's own
LE_QUERY move the OST to "lad_ost_phase2_list". Once the assistant
dequeues it to scan, the second handler inserts it back and the
OST is scanned again, as the orphans are not re-verified, they
could be repaired twice.

Skip the queueing once the target has been scanned: add a new flag
"ltd_layout_phase2_scanned" to protect it from scanning twice.

Also adding OBD_FAIL_LFSCK_DELAY3 in lfsck_layout_master_in_notify()
so test_18e hits the race deterministically.
