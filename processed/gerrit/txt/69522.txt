--- Commit Message ---
LU-20808 target: put out_xattr_list() result in its own slot

out_xattr_list() puts its result in the slot of the first update of
the request, rather than in the slot of its own update as the other
OUT handlers do. Its only sender, osp_md_xattr_list(), sends it on
its own, where the two are the same slot, so this has not shown. An
OUT_XATTR_LIST anywhere else in a request would overwrite the result
of the first update and leave its own empty.

Use the update's own index.
