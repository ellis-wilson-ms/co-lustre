LU-20531 tests: add reproducer for cross-MDT close deadlock

Add test_171 to recovery-small.sh that reproduces the LU-20531 deadlock:
generate OPEN-bit ibit locks on a remote MDT, inject
OBD_FAIL_LDLM_REPLAY_PAUSE (0x32e, fail_val=2), then fail the MDT.
Without the fix in mdc_close(), the import deadlocks at REPLAY_LOCKS.

This change is the test only (no fix) to demonstrate the failure on
unpatched code. The fix is in change 67599.
