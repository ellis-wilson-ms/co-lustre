LU-12669 ec: serialize concurrent EC recovery readers

CIT_EC_RD reconstructs missing data stripes from parity, holding the
recovered pages locked in ec_page_list until vvp_io_ec_rd_end().  When
two readers reach the same page concurrently the second crashed on the
cp_owner assert, and blocking on the first reader's locks deadlocks
(AB-BA: the recovery owner can fault in an mmap'ed user buffer that
maps a vmpage the spinner holds).

Add a CP_EC_RECOVERY flag to cl_page::cp_flags.  The reader that
starts recovering a page sets the flag while the vmpage is still
locked; a second reader finding it drops the vmpage lock and retries,
waiting for the owner to clear the flag in vvp_io_ec_rd_end().

The retry loop is bounded by EC_RECOVERY_RETRY_MAX (200 x 10ms sleeps
via schedule_timeout_killable(), ~2s per lov_ec_read_stripe_pages()
call).  On exhaustion it unwinds deliberately: it sets ci_need_restart
and returns -ENODATA, the same restart path ll_file_io_generic()
recognizes for the CIT_READ -> CIT_EC_RD switch; cl_io_end() then runs
vvp_io_ec_rd_end(), disowning every held vmpage and clearing the flag,
and ll_file_io_generic() restarts the IO from scratch -- releasing all
locks is what breaks the AB-BA cycle.  -EINTR is returned to userspace
as the IO has genuinely been interrupted.

-EAGAIN is deliberately *not* used for the exhaustion.  cl_io_loop()
maps -EAGAIN to -EIO when ci_tried_all_mirrors is set, but that bit
tracks mirror rounds (and drifts up with the partial-IO chunking of a
large read), not EC contention, so it would fail a read that parity
could still rebuild.  To stop the unwinding from looping forever while
a peer stays stuck in recovery, the number of such restarts is counted
in cl_io::ci_ec_restart_cnt, carried across restarts by
ll_file_io_generic() and reset there on forward progress (so chunking
a large read does not spend the budget); once it reaches
EC_RECOVERY_RESTART_MAX the read gives up with -EIO.

Ordering and atomicity notes:

* cp_flags is accessed with set_bit()/clear_bit()/test_bit() because
the clear in vvp_io_ec_rd_end() runs after cl_page_disown() has
unlocked the vmpage, while readahead may concurrently touch the
cp_defer_uptodate bitfield word.

* The recovery owner unlinks the page from ec_page_list with plain
stores (cl_page_list_del()) before clearing CP_EC_RECOVERY with the
unordered clear_bit().  A spinner that sees the flag clear and
immediately re-adds the page (LASSERT(list_empty(&page->cp_batch)))
could on a weakly ordered CPU observe the clear before the unlink.
smp_mb() before the Uptodate clear publishes the unlink first, and
smp_mb__before_atomic() before the clear_bit() orders the Uptodate
clear before the flag clear (write_sequnlock() is only a release for
the seqlock, not a store-store barrier for the clear_bit() that
follows).  Paired with the reader's smp_rmb() after its test_bit(), a
spinner that sees CP_EC_RECOVERY clear also sees both the unlink and
the !Uptodate state, so it neither trips the cp_batch LASSERT nor
misclassifies a just-invalidated page as EC_DPG_UPTODATE and serves
stale data.

* A plain ll_readpage() reads only PageUptodate(), never
CP_EC_RECOVERY, and on !Uptodate re-reads the page through
cl_page_list_add(), which LASSERTs list_empty(&page->cp_batch).  The
teardown must therefore not expose the page as !Uptodate while it is
still linked.  On the recovery-failed path the pages stay owned (the
early cl_page_disown() is skipped) and are unlinked before being
disowned, so a reader blocked on the vmpage lock observes the unlink
through the lock handoff; on the success path they were already
disowned (and are Uptodate) for generic_file_read_iter(), and the
smp_mb()/Uptodate-clear pair above is what publishes the unlink.

* Reconstructed pages have their PageUptodate cleared in
vvp_io_ec_rd_end(), forcing the next read through ll_readpage() for a
fresh DLM lock instead of serving stale data from the page cache.
The clear runs under lli_page_inv_lock, the same seqlock
vvp_page_delete() uses (LU-16160), so a reader in vvp_io_read_start()
or a fault in ll_filemap_fault() racing it sees the invalidation and
retries its short read / SIGBUS instead of returning it to userspace.

Also convert -EAGAIN returned by generic_file_read_iter() in
vvp_io_ec_rd_end() (DLM lock enqueue failure on an NDELAY stripe) into
an IO restart rather than leaking it to userspace, gated on
!ci_iocb_nowait so RWF_NOWAIT reads still get -EAGAIN.

Miscellany: cl_io_set_range() becomes static and rewording
coo_io_set_range() comment to reflect top-to-bottom iteration; cl_page
has a BUILD_BUG_ON guard on cp_flags width; sanity-ec.sh
concurrent-reader tests report per-reader failures and outputs.
