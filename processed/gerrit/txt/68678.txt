--- Commit Message ---
LU-20702 osp: don't free opd_update while LOD threads run

LOD sends LCFG_PRE_CLEANUP to its OSP targets before it stops the
update recovery threads.  That order is deliberate - the OSP
disconnect is what unblocks those threads (8299bdd484 "LU-6705 lod:
re-order lodsub recovery cleanup") - but osp_process_config() also ran
osp_update_fini() in that phase, freeing opd_update while a recovery
thread could still be writing to the OSP, as happens when an MDT mount
fails (recovery-small test_111):

 BUG: unable to handle kernel NULL pointer dereference at 0000000000000008
 RIP: osp_check_and_set_rpc_version+0x138 [osp]
  osp_md_write ... llog_cat_cleanup ... lod_sub_recovery_thread [lod]

Defer osp_update_fini() to LCFG_CLEANUP, which LOD only sends after
lod_sub_stop_recovery_threads() has joined those threads, and stop
reading opd_update twice in osp_check_and_set_rpc_version() and
osp_trans_stop().  This also covers the unwind paths that send
LCFG_CLEANUP without LCFG_PRE_CLEANUP, which used to leak opd_update
and leave the update thread running.
