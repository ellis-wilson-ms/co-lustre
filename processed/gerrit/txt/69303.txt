LU-14058 tests: fix more tests with more than 10 MDTs

Several test scripts outside sanity.sh open-code the MDT name as
"MDT000$idx" or with printf, and sanity-hsm.sh and sanity-pcc.sh
use MDT_PREFIX, which only works for MDT0000-MDT0009.  These
scripts fail, or refuse to run, with more than 10 MDTs configured.

Use mdtname_from_index() or ${MDT[]} to generate the MDT names, and
remove MDT_PREFIX and the MDSCOUNT limit from sanity-hsm.sh and
sanity-pcc.sh.

Except conf-sanity/66 and sanity-hsm/251, 254b and 500 with more
than 9 MDTs until they are fixed.

Test-Parameters: trivial
Test-Parameters: mdscount=2 mdtcount=12 testlist=sanity-hsm
Test-Parameters: mdscount=2 mdtcount=12 testlist=sanity-pcc
Test-Parameters: mdscount=2 mdtcount=12 testlist=conf-sanity
Test-Parameters: mdscount=2 mdtcount=12 testlist=replay-single
