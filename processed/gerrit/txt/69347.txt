LU-20853 build: Add systemd/systemd-dev to Build-Depends

Since systemd 253-2, systemd.pc has been moved to systemd-dev, so
without it configure can't find the unit dir and the deb packages
are built without the systemd unit files.

Add systemd-dev or systemd older than 253-2 to Build-Depends, and
recheck systemd.pc in debian/rules so that make debs/dkms-debs works
after installing systemd-dev without rerunning configure. Use the
nosystemd profile for --with-systemdsystemunitdir=no to skip both.
Also add a warning in configure as a reminder.
