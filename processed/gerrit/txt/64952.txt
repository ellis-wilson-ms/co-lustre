LU-20053 ldiskfs: fix iloc.bh leak in dec_ref_all

The commit c8e008b60492 ("ext4: ignore xattrs past end")
introduced a refcount leak when block_csum is false.

ext4_xattr_inode_dec_ref_all() calls ext4_get_inode_loc() to
get iloc.bh, but never releases it with brelse().

RHEL backported c8e008b60492 in 4.18.0-553.61.1.el8_10 and
5.14.0-570.23.1.el9_6, and every 9.7, 9.8 and 10.x kernel
Lustre supports has it. Older 8.10 kernels get a new series
without the fix, since it does not apply to them. The 9.6
series already needs 570.30.1 or later, so it always takes
the fix.
