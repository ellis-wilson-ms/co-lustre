--- Commit Message ---
LU-20264 llite: resolve new-inode owner on the client

Add op_owner_uid/op_owner_gid to md_op_data and fill them in from
ll_prep_md_op_data() for create-style operations via a new
ll_init_op_data_owner() helper, an inode_init_owner() mirror that
applies setgid-directory inheritance and idmap-aware S_ISGID
stripping (using vfsgid_in_group_p() and
capable_wrt_inode_uidgid() so the checks are correct for callers in
a non-init user namespace).

The create packers now consume the pre-resolved owner and the
possibly-adjusted op_mode instead of computing fresh from current's
raw fsuid/fsgid, so the MDS receives the final owner as the VFS
would have assigned it.
