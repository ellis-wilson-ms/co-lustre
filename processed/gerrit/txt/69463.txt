LU-20808 ptlrpc: RFC carry IT_MDT_TO_MDT_READ as OUT reads

This patch is only for review of the wire protocol and the data
structures, and should not land as it is.  If the approach is
accepted, it should be merged into the earlier LU-20808 patches
rather than applied at the end of the series:

  - 69254 "ptlrpc: add the MDT-to-MDT read intent wire format":
    define RQF_LDLM_INTENT_MDT_READ on RMF_OUT_UPDATE_HEADER and
    RMF_OUT_UPDATE_REPLY, without struct mdt_read_req/rep/xattr
  - 69255 "lod: add a raw xattr accessor": no longer needed
  - 69256 "osp: carry a lock intent on a remote lock enqueue":
    in-memory md_lock_intent, reads packed as OUT updates
  - 69257 "osp: seed the object cache from a lock intent reply":
    cache from the object_update_reply, size-only xattr entries
  - 69259, 69447 and 69260 (owner handler, xattrs, striped dirs):
    out_reads_check() and out_reads_run() in target/out_handler.c
  - 69261 to 69267 (callers): MDR_* flags become MLI_*

The MDT-to-MDT read intent defines a wire format of its own: request
and reply structs, a 208-byte obdo buffer, and a name-keyed xattr
bundle with a status per name.  The MDT owning the object
re-implements OUT_ATTR_GET, OUT_XATTR_GET and OUT_INDEX_LOOKUP
through MDD and LOD, with a new raw xattr accessor to get back to
the bytes OUT returns, and OSP parses the reply separately from the
OUT results it already caches.

Carry the intent's reads as an object_update_request of read-only
OUT updates of the locked object, inline in RMF_OUT_UPDATE_HEADER,
and return an object_update_reply.  The owner runs them with the OUT
handlers under the try-lock, without stopping at a failed read, and
the requester seeds its cache from the same results an OUT_UPDATE
would return.  No new wire structs are needed, another xattr or read
op needs no format change, and statuses are network errnos.

A value that does not fit in the room left comes back as its size
without the value, as OUT_XATTR_GET does for an empty buffer, and
OSP caches that size, so that reading a large default layout costs
one read instead of LOD's three (-ERANGE, size probe, read).

Also set LOHA_EXISTS when attributes are seeded, answer a read the
owner cannot run, or a read of another object, with an error of its
own instead of failing the enqueue, decline rather than fail an
intent with more reads than the owner runs, and refuse the intent in
an MDS_BATCH sub-request.  Also fix out_xattr_list() putting its
result in the first slot rather than its own.

mdbench on one VM with 2 MDTs, parent on MDT0 and child on MDT1,
3000 ops x 3 runs per build, builds alternated twice, ops/s with OUT
RPCs per op:

  op       typed intent          OUT reads
  mkdir    3550 +-77   2.88      3541 +-76   2.88
  rmdir    2714 +-153  1.16      2660 +-182  1.11
  rename   1792 +-113  6.01      1765 +-74   6.01
  unlink   2486 +-106  1.44      2451 +-49   1.45
