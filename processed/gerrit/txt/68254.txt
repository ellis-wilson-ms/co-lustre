--- Commit Message ---
LU-20348 tests: count only client-side ldlm namespaces

remount_client_normally() verifies that no ldlm namespace is left
over once the client has been remounted, but ldlm.namespaces.* also
matches the server-side namespaces. Where the client shares a node
with the servers the count can never reach MDSCOUNT+OSTCOUNT+1, so
every encryption subtest fails in cleanup. Count only the client
side namespaces, and log the whole list when the count is wrong.
