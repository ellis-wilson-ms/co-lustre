LU-20879 mdt: reject malformed MDS_BATCH requests

mdt_batch() trusts the buffer counts, sizes and sub request headers
that the client puts in an MDS_BATCH request, so a malformed request
is not always handled correctly.  Also fix missing swabbing of batch
sub requests.

Add tgt_update_bufs_get() to validate the buffer count and sizes
against the request before fetching the update buffers, and use it in
mdt_batch(), capping the total size at BUT_MAXREQSIZE and the bulk
fragment count.  Before running a sub request, check that it fits in
its buffer and swab its header, and require exactly buh_update_count
of them.

Also keep req_capsule_server_grow() from underflowing its limit when
the reply is already larger than BUT_MAXREPSIZE, and reject a buffer
length that round_up() in lustre_unpack_msg_v2() would wrap past the
limit.

Add sanity test_123ba, which fuzzes a fraction of the client batched
getattr RPCs with the new OBD_FAIL_BUT_CORRUPT_REQ and checks that the
MDS keeps serving.  OBD_FAIL_BUT_CORRUPT_REQ is numbered 0x171e and
placed after 0x1709 rather than at the end of the 0x170x block, so it
does not take a context conflict with other patches appending fail_loc
values there; a later patch can renumber it, and it leaves room for
intermediate values.
