--- Commit Message ---
LU-19770 llite: split ll_dir_ioctl into smaller chunks

ll_dir_ioctl() was over 900 lines long. Make it a dispatcher
that calls a helper for each ioctl, or for a group of ioctls
that share code, and handles one-line ioctls inline.

This also fixes a request leak: LL_IOC_LMV_GETSTRIPE returned
without releasing the request when the foreign LMV size was
invalid. Index ladvise_names with array_index_nospec() to
silence a spectre v1 warning.

The initial split was done with claude code + minimax 2.1.

Fixes: fdad38781ccc ("LU-11376 lmv: new foreign LMV format")
