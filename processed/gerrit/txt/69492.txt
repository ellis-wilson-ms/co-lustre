LU-9868 tests: rename through a stale directory dentry

A client that gives a directory inode a second hashed dentry
can make rename(2) take that directory's i_rwsem twice and
hang in D state, which was seen as racer test_2 hangs:
lock_rename() and its loop check compare dentries, so they
take the two dentries for different directories.

Add sanityn test_123 to form the second dentry on purpose.
The first mount holds its cwd in foo/bar, which keeps foo
and foo/bar hashed after the second mount moves bar out of
foo and then foo under bar. A rename of ../baz onto foo from
that cwd then reaches the source through the stale foo
dentry, since ".." is not revalidated, and looks up foo
under bar, where the client used to add a second dentry for
foo.
The rename runs in the background and the test fails if it
has not returned after 30 seconds.

Without the d_splice_alias() change for directories the
rename hangs in vfs_rename(); with it the lookup of foo
fails with ELOOP and the rename returns.

Based on the reproducer in https://review.whamcloud.com/65122
