LU-20333 mgs: Associate nodemaps with exports on connect/reconnect

This patch enables nodemap support on the MGS server by associating
nodemaps with client exports during the connection and reconnection
process.

This provides the foundation for MGS to leverage nodemap properties
for access control and identity mapping, enabling more granular
security policies on the management server.
