--- Commit Message ---
LU-20720 osd-ldiskfs: read the inode table, not the inodes

The scan called iget() on every object to reach its attributes.
iget() takes the inode hash lock, builds a VFS inode and puts it
on the LRU, and none of that is wanted by a walk that reads each
object once and never looks at it again.

osd_inode_getattr() decodes the on-disk inode out of the inode
table block the iterator already holds, so a DOIF_ATTR walk that
wants attributes reads no more than a walk that wants FIDs.  The
iget path stays for the callers that need a real inode.
