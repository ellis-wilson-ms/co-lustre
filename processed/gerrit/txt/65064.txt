--- Commit Message ---
LU-19921 target: add gssiam_server tunables in tgt_lproc.c

This patch adds the new sptlrpc.gssiam_server kobject and target
GSSIAM tunables (gssiam_auth_upcall, gssiam_auth_downcall,
gssiam_refresh_interval, gssiam_flush), along with the helper
tgt_gssiam_downcall() in tgt_gssiam.c and its sysfs wrapper in
tgt_lproc.c.

It also moves existing target-level tunables (LUSTRE_RW_ATTR and
LUSTRE_WO_ATTR) from tgt_main.c into tgt_lproc.c to keep
lprocfs/sysfs code in its own file, converting sprintf() calls to
scnprintf() along the way.

Seven new manual pages are added (gssiam_auth_upcall.4,
gssiam_auth_downcall.4, gssiam_refresh_interval.4, gssiam_flush.4,
sync_lock_cancel.4, tgt_fmd_count.4, tgt_fmd_seconds.4), and
module-specific redirects/symlinks are updated accordingly.
