--- Commit Message ---
LU-0000 llite: parallel creates via d_lookup_done

Release the directory inode lock early in ll_atomic_open() when
O_CREAT is set, allowing parallel file creates in the same directory
to proceed concurrently instead of serializing on the dir mutex.
Call d_lookup_done() before re-acquiring the lock on return to
signal completion of the parallel dentry lookup.  For kernels
without DCACHE_PAR_LOOKUP, provide a no-op stub in lustre_compat.h.

Additionally, remove -ENODEV from the retriable conditions in
osp_precreate_reserve() and osp_precreate_ready_condition().  When
an OST disconnects, callers were left sleeping for up to obd_timeout
seconds.  With many parallel creates in flight this causes all
threads to block until the full timeout fires.  Returning immediately
on -ENODEV allows LOD to skip the disconnected OST and assign
stripes to the remaining available targets.
