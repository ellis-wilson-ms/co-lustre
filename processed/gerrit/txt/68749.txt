LU-19605 tests: Take nid1 off the wire in both directions

sanity-lnet test_236 sets the health of nid1 to 0. The test then
expects the router to show the peer NI for nid1 as down. But the
test drops only the local recovery pings of nid1. The router still
pings nid1, and nid1 still replies. The router then marks the peer
NI up again in lnet_parse(). The call to check_remote_peer_ni_status
sees "up", and the test fails at random.

The inbound ping is also a problem on the local node. LNet calls
lnet_inc_ni_healthv() for the receive NI of every good message. This
call raises the health of nid1 above 0.

Take nid1 off the wire in both directions. Drop every message that
nid1 sends, and drop every message that the local net sends to nid1.
