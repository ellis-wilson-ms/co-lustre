--- Commit Message ---
LU-13188 lctl: add -q option to 'lctl set_param'

Add the --quiet option to 'lctl set_param' to disable printing of
information. This is an alias for the --no-name option.

Also accept "--no-name" for set_param, matching what is already
done for get_param/list_param/find_param, and what the man page and
usage string document. Keep accepting the historical "--noname"
spelling for backwards compatibility.
