--- Commit Message ---
LU-20689 utils: Support decimal (SI) size units

llapi_parse_size() took a trailing "B" as decoration, so "1GB"
meant 2^30 while the kernel's own sysfs parser reads it as 10^9,
and a loose suffix such as "1Gijunk" was taken as 1GiB.  Read a
trailing "B" as 10^3-based, keep "G" and "GiB" 2^10-based, and
validate the whole suffix in one helper.

Do so only where the size is not part of a file layout: bandwidth
limits, 'lfs find --blocks' and '--size', ladvise extents, quota
limits, and the copytool and llsom_sync buffers.  A layout size
has to stay a multiple of 64KiB, so "-E", "-S" and "-z" keep
reading a "B" as decoration.

Scaling a fraction as "frac * size_units / frac_d" also overflowed
once the unit was large, so "1.999999999999999999G" came out 1GiB
short.  Scale it after the unit is known, one digit at a time from
the last, and refuse what still does not fit.
