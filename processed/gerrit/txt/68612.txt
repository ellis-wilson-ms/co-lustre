Some concerns, see inline comments. The main one is whether this actually addresses LU-20086: time(2) and clock_gettime(CLOCK_REALTIME) can only differ by a fraction of a timer tick, but the failures in the ticket ('-mtime 2w', '-mtime 1d', '-mtime 4h', '-mtime -1y') go through set_time(), which leaves fp_time_margin at 86400/86400/3600 seconds respectively, and find_value_cmp() compares with that margin. A sub-tick shift in `t` cannot flip any of those comparisons, which matches the ticket's own observation that this does not look like a small timestamp race and that it reproduces only on aarch64 clients. Could the real problem be on the timestamp side (what the client/MDT reports for stx_mtime) rather than in how lfs computes 'now'?
