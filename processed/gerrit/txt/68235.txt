LU-9653 utils: fix up changelog kernel-doc

Give every documented function in liblustreapi_chlg.c a "Return:"
section, since kernel-doc only recognises the keyword with a colon,
and convert the leftover doxygen and plain comments on
llapi_changelog_recv(), _fini() and _free().  Use %-errno or the
actual %-EINVAL rather than the non-symbol %negative, and realign the
@mask continuation lines under their new prefix.
changelog_parse_username() has no failure path, so document that it
always returns 0.  DEFAULT_RECORD_FMT moves above the new block
because kernel-doc binds it to the next prototype.
