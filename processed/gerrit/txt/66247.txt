--- Commit Message ---
LU-19507 nodemap: add immutable_flags RBAC

Introduce immutable_flags nodemap RBAC role
(NODEMAP_RBAC_IMMUTABLE_FLAGS) to gate inode attributes changes.
IMMUTABLE/APPEND updates in mdd_fix_attr() are enforced; the RBAC
check runs before capability checks, so mapped root without this
role is denied even with CAP_LINUX_IMMUTABLE.

Keep wire-constants/tests/doc in sync, trusted internal ucred paths
explicitly bypassed as well.

Add new test sanity-sec/test_64l.
