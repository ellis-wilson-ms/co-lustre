LU-20264 llite: plumb mnt_idmap through setattr

Pass the VFS-provided mnt_idmap through ll_setattr_raw(),
ll_md_setattr() and cl_setattr_ost(). VFS setattr translates
ia_vfsuid/ia_vfsgid back into the filesystem user namespace before
building the RPC, the TIMES_SET permission check uses the caller's
idmap, and truncate credentials sent to the OST are mapped through
it. Internal callers (HSM import, futimes, flag updates) use the
identity mapping.
