No functional concerns: stripping all whitespace from both sides of the diff leaves the 243 removed and 245 added lines identical apart from two added blank lines, so this is provably a pure reformat.

The concern is the approach. These two files are imported Intel ISA-L sources, and de7b67d536 ("LU-19465 ec: add erasure code as a separate library") states the reason they are kept in upstream formatting: "The files are mostly un-modified to make it easy to verify and compare them to upstream, or just dropping in new/updated versions when required." That has held so far -- between that import and this patch the only deltas were the include-path rewrites and the __riscv guard in af31322390. Reformatting every line of executable code gives up the ability to diff against ISA-L and turns any future re-import into a whole-file conflict. Is the checkpatch noise worth that? contrib/scripts/gerrit_checkpatch.py already has a CHECKPATCH_IGNORED_FILES list (currently the generated wiretest.c files) that would exempt lustre/ec/ec_base*.c at no churn.
