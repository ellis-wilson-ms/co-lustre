--- Commit Message ---
LU-6142 ptlrpc: fix cpu index computation in cpu_latency_work()

latency_qos and cpus_latency_qos are both struct cpu_latency_qos
pointers, so their difference is already the element index.  Dividing
that by sizeof(struct cpu_latency_qos) a second time yields a bogus cpu
number in the CDEBUG() messages.  Use the pointer difference directly.
