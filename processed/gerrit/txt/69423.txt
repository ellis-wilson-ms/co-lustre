LU-9 lod: allocate MDT stripes by weighted round-robin

The stripes of a new striped directory are allocated on MDTs chosen
at random, weighted by their free space and inodes less a penalty
for recently used MDTs and MDSs.  Like the random OST allocation
before, this spreads the stripes unevenly over short runs, and the
penalties need tuning to work against the randomness.

Allocate the remote MDT stripes in lod_mdt_alloc_qos() by weighted
round-robin over the round-robin array, like OST objects.  The same
ltd_qos_wrr_weights() adjusts the weights by mdt_qos_prio_free and
caps them so that no MDT gets more than one stripe of a directory.
The MDT penalties are no longer used.  Leave the existing stripes of a
directory being split out of the weights, and skip inactive MDTs,
which are not in the round-robin array.

Add lod.*.mdt_qos_wrr_enable to fall back to picking the MDTs at
random by weight, as for OSTs.

Update the man pages for the MDT parameters.  Add sanity test_413m to
check the balance of the remote stripes over the MDTs.

Also refresh the OSP statfs data soon when osp.*.maxage is lowered,
instead of after the old, longer interval.  A long qos_maxage set by
test_413m otherwise left "df" with stale MDT statfs data for an hour
after it was restored, and sanity test_418 failed.

Creating 300 2-stripe directories on 4 MDTs, with stripe 0 placed
round-robin by the client and mdt_qos_threshold_rr=0, gives these
second stripes per MDT in total (wanted 75, or about 67 on MDT3 with
15% fewer free inodes) and per 30 directories (wanted 7-8):

                            before            after
                            total  per 30     total  per 30
  equal MDTs                69-81  3-12       74-76  6-9
  MDT3 15% fewer inodes     65-84  3-13       67-79  5-10
