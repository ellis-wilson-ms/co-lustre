--- Commit Message ---
LU-10026 csdc: add compression header checksum

This commit adds functionality to verify and calculate the checksum
of the header in the `ll_compr_hdr` struct and some another sanity
check.

The client sets LLCH_FL_HDR_CSUM in llch_flags when it stores the
header checksum, and the checksum is only verified when that flag
is set. A chunk with a valid magic and checksum but inconsistent
header fields is treated as corrupted and the read fails with
-EUCLEAN; without a valid checksum such a chunk is passed through
as plain data.

Add sanity-compr test_1080, which exercises these checks with a
pre-built compressed sample (lustre/tests/compressed.bin).
