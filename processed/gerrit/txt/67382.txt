LU-20496 efalnd: Check conn destroy eligibility under conn lock

To decide whether a connection is ready to be destroyed or not, we check
if the active/abort Tx lists hold objects or the lists are empty which
means the connection is idle. Those lists are updated under the
connection lock so we must read them under the same lock.
Also add the pending Tx list to the check, which means we check all
lists a Tx can be on while is owned by the connection promising we
destroy it only when truly idle.
