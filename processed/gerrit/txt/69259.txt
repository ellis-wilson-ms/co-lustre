LU-20808 mdt: handle IT_MDT_TO_MDT_READ on the owning MDT

Handle IT_MDT_TO_MDT_READ: grant the lock the enqueue asks for and,
while doing so, run the OUT reads it carries on the locked object,
with the handlers that serve them in OUT_UPDATE. out_reads_check()
checks and swabs them before the lock is taken, and out_reads_run()
runs them under it. Only the lookup of a name and the attributes are
run for now: any other read, xattrs included, is answered -EPROTO,
and the requester reads it itself. A read that fails does not stop
the reads after it, and one of another object than the locked one,
or with a malformed name, is refused on its own.

Nothing is checked on the requester's behalf. The name is resolved
without a permission check, which the requester makes itself, with
its own view of the user, from the attributes and ACL that come back.
So no credentials travel, and the two MDTs cannot come to disagree
about a user.

The handler runs in the mdt_out service, which must not be held up,
so the lock is granted only if it is free at once. Waiting for it
would hold an mdt_out thread until whoever has the lock lets go, and
that operation may need an mdt_out thread on this MDT to finish, for
an OUT_UPDATE of its own transaction. So the lock's bits are asked for
as try bits, which ldlm grants at once or not at all, without queueing
the lock or calling in a conflicting one. When not all of them are
granted, nothing is read and the handler returns without
DISP_IT_EXECD, leaving ldlm to grant the lock the ordinary way; the
requester then reads what it needs itself. A resend is treated the
same way, and so is an intent with more reads than OUT_READS_MAX or a
reply too large to go inline, so that a newer requester asking for
more does not fail. For the same reason the object is looked up with
LOC_F_LOCAL: a FID that is not this MDT's fails at once, instead of
waiting on the MDT that owns it.

The intent is accepted only from another MDT that agreed on it: the
export must have OBD_CONNECT_MDS_MDS and OBD_CONNECT2_DNE_INTENT, and
under GSS the request must be authenticated as coming from an MDT,
since the connect flags alone are the peer's own claim. It is refused
in an MDS_BATCH sub-request, which does not have its format. What is
read must also be what the lock covers: each read has to name the
lock resource's FID.

The lock is granted even when a read fails, as each result has its
own status. The lock taken is exactly the resource and policy the
requester asked for: the name-hash resource cannot be recomputed here,
because mdt_lock_pdo_mode() consults the local device and may answer
differently than it did on the requester.

Advertise OBD_CONNECT2_DNE_INTENT, and have LOD ask for it on its
MDT-to-MDT connections.
