--- Commit Message ---
LU-19852 lod: raidset aware stripe allocator

Make the qos and rr stripe allocators raidset aware.

For data allocations:
- -c: prefer unique OSTs for all data stripes (multi-raidset mins may
  be relaxed; geometry can shrink when OSTs are short)
- -C: require unique OSTs within each raidset; OSTs may be reused
  across raidsets via overstriping

For EC data components without overstriping (-c), both lod_ost_alloc_qos()
and lod_ost_alloc_rr() may reduce the requested data stripe count when at
least 75% of the request can still be placed, then resplit the geometry.
Parity and overstriped (-C) components are not rescaled this way.

For parity allocations:
Parities can reuse OSTs selected for data stripes as long as they are
reused in a different raidset.
