--- Commit Message ---
LU-17000 llite: drop a dead NULL check on inode

ll_dom_finish_open() dereferences inode in its variable initializers,
before any of its checks, so the later "inode &&" guard cannot be what
prevents a fault.  Both callers take that inode from ll_prep_inode(),
which on success either leaves the caller's non-NULL inode untouched or
stores an ll_iget() result it has already checked with IS_ERR(), and
ll_iget() returns an ERR_PTR() rather than NULL for every failure.

Reported by smatch as "variable dereferenced before check 'inode'".
