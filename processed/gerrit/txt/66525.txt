--- Commit Message ---
LU-19079 krb: introduce lustre_role principal

Introduce support for new client Kerberos principal in the form:
lustre_role/NODEMAP_NAME@REALM

This principal does not have a hostname, so it is valid for use by any
client node. But it is only accepted by Lustre servers if the nodemap
this principal refers to has the gss identification property set.
