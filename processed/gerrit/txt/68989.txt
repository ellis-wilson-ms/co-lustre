--- Commit Message ---
LU-20645 utils: add --printf and --ls opts to lfs-getstripe

Add --printf and --ls options to lfs-getstripe matching the behavior
of corresponding lfs-find options, for printing specific fields of a
file's layout.

In order to reuse lfs-find code, the following functions have been
exposed in lustre/utils/lustreapi_internal.h: printf_format_string(),
get_projid(), and convert_lmd_statx().

Includes documentation and tests for output parity with lfs-find and
symlink behavior during recursive traversal.

Options which select layouts, components, or mirrors (-O, -D,
--mirror-*, and -I/-E/--comp-* with an argument) are rejected with
--printf/--ls, suggesting lfs find instead.
