--- Commit Message ---
LU-19575 tests: retry umount of PCC backend device

sanity-pcc tests intermittently fail in cleanup with "umount:
/mnt/pcc.*: target is busy" (rc 32) even though lsof just
reported the mount idle. The backend mount might be held briefly
by references lsof cannot see: the just terminated copytool
still releasing its files on exit, or kernel-side PCC mount
references that are dropped asynchronously after detach.

umount_loopdev() waits for lsof to report the mount idle but
attempts the umount only once, so any transient hold fails the
test. Retry the umount itself instead. A leaked reference
still fails the last attempt.
