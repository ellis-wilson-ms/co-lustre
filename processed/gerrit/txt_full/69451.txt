LU-20872 build: Honor o2ib configuration in packaging

1) configure: add the default fallback to the OFED search dir,
   disable external (not in-kernel) o2iblnd on multiple OFED
   stacks, skip the external checks once it is disabled, and
   do not force EXTERNAL_KO2IBLND="yes" after detection.

2) rpm/deb: pass --with-o2ib from configure_args/IB_OPTIONS as
   is, and check after configure that the o2iblnd drivers match
   the mofed/o2ib bconds or ext_o2ib/noint_o2ib profiles.
