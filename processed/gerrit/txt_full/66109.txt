LU-20299 tests: Replace sanity test 833 with sanityn test 117

sanity/833 runs on a single client; tracing shows zero
osc_lock_discard_pages() entries during the whole run, so the protection
it claims to verify is never exercised. Even with
ci_invalidate_page_cache forced to 0, sanity/833 passes. This test
instead drives a real cross-client conflict and a fault-widened reader
so the discard path actually fires against an in-flight read.
