--- Commit Message ---
LU-19825 lustre: Use io_wait_event() instead of wait_event()

For cases that are I/O related, we should use io_wait_event() instead
of wait_event().

This allows the kernel to account sleeping processes waiting for IO
rather than waiting for the task to be runnable. That helps with
vmstat, top and other commands that distinguish how processes are
sleeping.
