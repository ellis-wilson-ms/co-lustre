LU-10026 tests: move verify_comp_attr to test-framework

Move verify_mirror_count() and verify_comp_attr() from sanity-flr.sh
to test-framework.sh so that other suites can use them, and teach
verify_comp_attr() the compression attributes (compr-type,
compr-level, compr-chunk) as well as pattern and mirror-id.

sanity-pfl test_100k already uses verify_comp_attr(), and the
following compression mirror tests need it too.
