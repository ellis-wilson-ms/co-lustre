LU-20763 nodemap: delete only the fileset records in use

nodemap_idx_fileset_clear() wipes the whole subid range reserved for a
fileset, instead of deleting the records the fileset really occupies.
Every fileset removal declares LUSTRE_NODEMAP_FILESET_SUBID_RANGE
(256) index deletes in a single transaction, whatever the length of
the fileset path, and even when the nodemap holds no fileset at all. A
nodemap with K alternate filesets pays K + 1 such transactions when it
is deleted.

On ldiskfs each index delete is declared with 4 credits, so one
removal declares 1024. When that exceeds the maximum transaction size
of the journal, osd_trans_start() clamps the transaction and
osd_trans_exec_op() complains:

  lustre-MDT0000: opcode 9: before 516 < left 1024, rollback = 8

To fix this, make nodemap_idx_fileset_clear() build the fileset info
and hand it to nodemap_idx_fileset_del().  As a consequence, fragments
orphaned by an interrupted 'fileset add' are not collected any more by
an unrelated nodemap removal. But wiping 256 records on every removal
was a poor way to garbage collect those.
