--- Commit Message ---
LU-20458 utils: Reject '.' in pool name

A pool name containing '.' injects a spurious level into the lctl
parameter hierarchy (e.g. qmt.test-QMT0000.dt-local.abc.glb-usr
for pool "local.abc"), so list_param/get_param cannot tell where
the pool name ends and a real sub-parameter begins.
