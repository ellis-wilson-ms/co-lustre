--- Commit Message ---
LU-20276 tests: exclude sanityn/56b due to OOM crash

Exclude sanityn.sh test_56b testing on clients with less than 15.5GB
of RAM until ZFS cache usage is fixed.  This threshold was chosen
somewhat arbitrarily, but will skip the current 4GB test VMs and
hopefully will still allow the test to run and pass with larger OSS.

Otherwise, it regularly causes the OSS to crash with OOM shortly after
test_56b deletes the large/sparse test file (in test_70b or test_71a
usually) while the OST objects are being destroyed.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanityn fstype=zfs
