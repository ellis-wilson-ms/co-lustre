--- Commit Message ---
LU-20566 ec: recover O_DIRECT reads with transient pages

Keep sync DIO EC recovery off the page cache: reconstruct into
CPT_TRANSIENT pages and copy_page_to_iter() to the caller. Fall
back to the buffered path when native allocation or encryption
bounce pages are unavailable. AIO EC recovery remains unsupported.

Successful and failed data-stripe submits keep non-owning
out_pages[] aliases for reconstruction; ownership stays on the
page list and lov_ec_forget_listed_pages() drops the aliases in
rg_cleanup (buffered and DIO share this rule).
