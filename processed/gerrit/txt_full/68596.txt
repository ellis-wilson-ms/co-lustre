--- Commit Message ---
LU-17000 ldlm: clear sysfs pointers in ldlm_cleanup()

ldlm_cleanup() unregisters ldlm_ns_kset, ldlm_svc_kset and ldlm_kobj
but leaves all three pointers referring to the freed objects.  Nothing
else resets them, so they only change when a later ldlm_setup() gets
far enough to reassign them.  An ldlm_setup() that fails before that
point runs ldlm_cleanup() on the values left over from the previous
mount and unregisters already freed ksets, which the kernel reports as
"refcount_t: underflow; use-after-free" out of kobject_put().

Clear each pointer as it is released.  kset_unregister() ignores a NULL
argument, so the two guards it had are dropped; ldlm_kobj keeps its one
because sysfs_remove_group() dereferences the kobject.
