Remove the llapi_file_open_volatile_layout() #define before it
ships in a release: it had no users and no man page, and one name,
llapi_layout_file_open_volatile(), matching llapi_layout_file_open()
and llapi_layout_file_create(), is enough.

Align the llapi_file_open_volatile_param() synopsis continuation
lines with its opening parenthesis, and indent the man page example
with spaces only, since tabs inside .nf render at different stops.
