--- Commit Message ---
LU-20464 ldlm: fix use-after-put in ldlm_flock_deadlock

In ldlm_flock_deadlock(), the code accesses bl_exp->exp_flock_hash
after calling class_export_put(bl_exp). If the put drops the last
reference, the export may be destroyed by the zombie workqueue
before the hash access, causing a use-after-free.

Reorder the operations to access the hash while the reference is
still held.

Fixes: 6a073773939c ("LU-1601 ldlm: Fix flock detection for different mounts")
