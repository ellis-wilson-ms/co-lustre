LU-20107 nrs: share one ceiling per CPT between both heads

A global rule for both queues divides its rate by 2 * ncpts. The high
priority head gets an I/O RPC only for a blocking callback. Half of
the node rate then stays idle. A divisor of ncpts is not correct
either. Each head has its own clock, so a partition can pass twice its
share.

Give the node class one limit clock and one reserved clock for each
CPU partition. Both heads of the partition use that pair, and the
divisor is ncpts. The reserved clock keeps the lag bound of mClock.

Add sanityn/test_77xj, and document the node tier in nrs_policies.4.

Squash the clock pair into the node tier change after review.
