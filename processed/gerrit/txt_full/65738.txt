--- Commit Message ---
LU-20229 kernel: disable CONFIG_CRYPTO_USER_API_AEAD

The most current kernels for RHEL 8.10, 9.7, and 10.1
contain the fix for the CVE-2026-31431 "Copy Fail" issue.
However, old kernels do not have this fix. We need to
disable the CONFIG_CRYPTO_USER_API_AEAD option at
kernel-config level to mitigate the issue.

Additional related options commonly disabled for hardening are:

CONFIG_CRYPTO_USER_API
CONFIG_CRYPTO_USER_API_HASH
CONFIG_CRYPTO_USER_API_SKCIPHER
CONFIG_CRYPTO_USER_API_RNG
