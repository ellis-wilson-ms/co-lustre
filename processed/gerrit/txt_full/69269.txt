LU-20808 tests: count rename and unlink RPCs across MDTs

Add sanity tests for the rename and unlink lock intents, and for the
permission checks the requesting MDT makes from the parent state an
intent brings back:

 1000f  mkdir under a remote parent is allowed or denied just as under
        a local one: on a group the client claims, on none, and on one
        only the identity upcall grants, with and without the upcall;
        rename and unlink are allowed on a claimed group
 1000g  a file renamed from a remote parent, back again, and onto an
        existing file keeps its data, owner, mode, xattr and ACL,
        within the RPC budget of each shape
 1000h  rmdir, rmdir of a directory that is not empty, and unlink of a
        file and of one of its hard links, under a remote parent
 1000i  directories created in and removed from a parent striped over
        both MDTs

1000f makes directories with "lfs mkdir", which leaves the permission
check to the MDS, so that a denial comes from the MDS and not from
the client. 1000g renames between directories right under the root,
where the ancestor walks that order the rename locks stop; deeper,
they read every ancestor on the other MDT, which no intent covers.

Test-Parameters: mdtcount=4 mdscount=2 testlist=sanity env=ONLY=1000
