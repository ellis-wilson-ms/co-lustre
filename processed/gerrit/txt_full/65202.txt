--- Commit Message ---
LU-19768 lnet: add LNET_CMD_STATS for global traffic counters

Add a new LNET_CMD_STATS generic netlink command that exports the
global LNet counters (message counts, byte counts, errors, allocation
high-water marks and the health counters) via the lnet_family. The
keys are named and ordered as "lnetctl stats show" already reports
them, so the command is able to replace the ioctl that backs it.

Also move the memset of lnet_counters_common out of the locked helper
into the caller, and guard against reading counters when LNet is not
running.
