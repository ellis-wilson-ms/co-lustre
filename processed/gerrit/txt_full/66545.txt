LU-19179 llite: add back flock flag macro check

This patch adds back flock flag macro check for F_SETLKW64,
F_SETLK64 and F_GETLK64 and put them into lustre_compat.
Otherwise, it will cause build failure on some distros (such as
arm64 rhel9.4 kernel).
