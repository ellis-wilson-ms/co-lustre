--- Commit Message ---
LU-18242 utils: avoid lengthy 'lfs df -m/-o=N' loop

When an explicit MDT or OST index is given to 'lfs df', it skips other
MDT or OST indexes that do not match the requested one.  However, when
the requested index is hit it continues in the index loop iteration
until the maximum value is hit, rather than exiting immediately.

Ensure that the loop terminates once the index is hit.  There is no
way to test this easily, but I verified with GDB that this now works.
