--- Commit Message ---
LU-9827 tests: sanityn/80b to wait for background process

'kill -9 $migrate_pid' in cleanup_80b() doesn't kill 'lfs migrate',
it kills the subshell only, so we still have our cleanup
procedure (with 'rm -rf') racing with 'lfs migrate'.
use an extra file to signal the subshell to complete.
