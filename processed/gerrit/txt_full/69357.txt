LU-20763 nodemap: split IAM batches into small transactions

nodemap_idx_insert_batch() and nodemap_idx_delete_batch() update any
number of nodemap IAM records in a single transaction. On ldiskfs each
IAM insert is declared with 16 credits and each delete with 4, so a
batch easily declares more credits than the maximum transaction size
of a small MGS journal:
- every fileset removal wipes the whole subid range reserved for the
  fileset, i.e. LUSTRE_NODEMAP_FILESET_SUBID_RANGE (256) deletes and
  1024 credits;
- a PATH_MAX long fileset is stored as 147 fragments, i.e. 2352
  credits to add it and 588 to delete it.

osd_trans_start() then clamps the transaction and osd_trans_exec_op()
complains:

  lustre-MDT0000: opcode 9: before 516 < left 1024, rollback = 8

To fix this, split batches into transactions of at most 16 inserts or
64 deletes, about 256 credits each. Rest of record processing stays
as is, so the undo logic of the callers is unchanged.

Add sanity-sec test_27g to check that fileset updates on the MGS do
not exceed the journal transaction size.
