--- Commit Message ---
LU-20519 llite: add some opencache stats

It can be difficult to understand if opencache is effective
without some stats. Add llite.*.open_cache_stats to count the
opens served from a cached open handle, the opens that needed
an open RPC, and the open lock requests triggered by the
opencache_threshold_count and opencache_threshold_ms tunables:

  # lctl get_param llite.*.open_cache_stats
  llite.lustre-ffff888150068000.open_cache_stats=
  snapshot_time             1790789687.052634017 secs.nsecs
  start_time                1790789686.511679375 secs.nsecs
  elapsed_time              0.540954642 secs.nsecs
  hits                      2 samples [reqs]
  misses                    1 samples [reqs]
  threshold_count_locks     1 samples [reqs]

Add sanity test_429b to check the counters, and a man page for
the new parameter.
