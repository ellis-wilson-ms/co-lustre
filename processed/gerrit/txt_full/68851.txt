--- Commit Message ---
LU-17296 tests: prove the mClock jobid+nid class buckets

The mClock jobid+nid key needs no client configuration. A client with
no job ID gets one class bucket for its node. A client with a job ID
gets one bucket for each job and node pair.

Add sanityn/test_77xh. The test reads the class bucket keys from the
D_RPCTRACE log of the OSS. With jobid_var=procname_uid, it finds two
job IDs and one NID. With jobid_var=disable, it finds one bucket. An
fio pair of two job IDs must then stay within a ratio of 1.4.

Document the jobid+nid key in nrs_policies.4.
