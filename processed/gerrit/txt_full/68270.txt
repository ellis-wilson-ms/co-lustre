LU-17115 quota: fix race of acquiring locks in qmt

In qmt_delete_qid and qmt_reset_qid, the order to require
the lock of lquota_entry and journal is different from that
in qmt_dqacq0, which could cause deadlock in some cases.

The qmt_reset_qid hunk of the original patch is omitted here,
as that function was introduced after b2_15 and does not exist
in this branch.  Only qmt_delete_qid is affected on b2_15.

This deadlock was hit on 2.15.8 with osd-zfs, where the two
threads wait on the ZFS transaction group instead of the
ldiskfs journal reported in the original ticket:

  qmt_delete_qid holds lqe_write_lock and waits for the txg
    qmt_quotactl -> qmt_delete_qid -> osd_trans_stop
      -> txg_wait_synced -> cv_wait
  qmt_dqacq0 holds an open tx handle and waits for lqe_write_lock
    qmt_dqacq -> qmt_dqacq0 -> qmt_trans_start_with_slv
      -> osd_trans_start -> dmu_tx_assign -> dmu_tx_wait
  txg_quiesce_thread waits for the open tx handles to drain
    txg_quiesce_thread -> cv_wait

The MDT pool stayed in the quiescing state and every quota
request piled up behind lqe_write_lock, leaving the MDT
unresponsive until the node was reset.
