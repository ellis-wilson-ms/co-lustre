LU-20600 osp: Add osp.*.precreate_state

When an OST crosses the reserved_mb_low or reserved_ino_low watermark
the OSP stops precreating objects on it, and resumes only once the
corresponding *_high watermark is crossed back, but both transitions
are only reported at CDEBUG(D_INFO) level.  osp.*.prealloc_status
gives the current state alone, so polling it tells neither when a
transition happened nor which space or inode value caused it.  Keep
the last 16 transitions per OSP device, each with its time, direction
and the values compared against the watermarks, and expose them as a
read-only osp.*.precreate_state:

  lctl get_param osp.*.precreate_state

Document it in Documentation/man4/osp.precreate_state.4.
