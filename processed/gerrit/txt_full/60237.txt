--- Commit Message ---
LU-13139 obdclass: make several dt functions exportable

Several dt functions are currently simple inline macros. Soon
these functions will expand with a follow on patch to include
ktime functions. Those functions are MODULE_EXPORT_GPL_ONLY
which will not work with osd-zfs. To prevent breaking osd-zfs
change these inline dt functions into obdclass functions that
are exportable.
