LU-20173 tests: filter on nettype when building nidlist

h2name_or_ip() returns NIDs for all valid addresses configured on a
host. It needs to filter on the NETTYPE being used, otherwise it
returns invalid NIDs such as <ip over ib address>@tcp.
This is particulary important since we now have in our testing
environment nodes with both Eth and IB network adapters.
