--- Commit Message ---
LU-20820 tests: fix test_133 pool_add decimal/hex mismatch

test_133 passed the decimal $poolostrange into OST[0-N], which
get_array_idx() parses as hexadecimal while the step is decimal.
With OSTCOUNT >= 12 pool_add then tried to add OST000f, which the test
means to leave out of the pool, and the nonexistent OST0010+, so the
test failed.

Use pool_add_targets() to add OSTs 0 through $poolostrange, which
converts the indices to hexadecimal and waits for the pool to reach
the LODs and client LOV.  Reuse the generated target list for
pool_remove, and document that range bounds are hexadecimal while the
step is decimal.

Fixes: e642e75cde02 ("LU-13363 lod: do object allocation in OST pool")
