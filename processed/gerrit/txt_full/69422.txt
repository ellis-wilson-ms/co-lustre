LU-8933 lod: leave inactive targets out of the RR array

Inactive OSTs stay in the round-robin array, so every layout that
reaches one skips it and uses up an index, and the OST after it in
the array gets its share of first stripes as well as its own.

Leave inactive targets out of the array built by lod_qos_calc_rr(),
unless all of them are inactive, and rebuild the arrays when a target
becomes active or inactive, counted in lq_rr_gen along with failure
domain changes.  The RR allocators index the array by its own size
rather than the pool's.  The periodic statfs update still checks the
inactive targets, and notices when they become active again.  OSTs
that are full, read-only, or have max_create_count=0 are inactive for
this too, so they also leave the array until they can be used again.
This also stops directory stripes being allocated on a deactivated
MDT, which lod_mdt_alloc_rr() and lod_mdt_alloc_qos() did not check
for.  Keep returning -EINPROGRESS when no target could be used and one
of the pool is still connecting, or became active since the array was
built, and don't let the smaller array raise the rounds of an
overstriped directory above lod_max_stripes_per_mdt.  Overstriped OST
layouts are likewise spread over the active OSTs only, e.g. "lfs
setstripe -C 8" with 1 of 8 OSTs inactive gets 8 stripes with 2 on one
OST, instead of 7 stripes.

Rebuilding the arrays after a target becomes active or inactive only
tries to take lq_rw_sem, and the allocators keep using the old array,
so creates don't wait behind allocators sleeping in precreate during
an OST failover (LU-15393).  Only if the array stays stale for 10 *
qos_maxage, or at once and then every qos_maxage after a target
becomes active, does one thread wait at most 2s for it, as QoS
allocation does, with that timed wait moved to lod_qos_write_lock().
Count the targets that become active in lq_active_gen for this.

Add sanity test_51i to check the first stripe balance with OST0001
deactivated.  With 8 OSTs and 800 2-stripe files it gives these first
stripes per active OST:

  before  50-200
  after   113-115
