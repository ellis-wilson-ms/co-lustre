LU-12669 tests: force OSTCOUNT=6 for sanity-compr

Increase OSTCOUNT to 6 in sanity-compr if it is lower so that it
has proper test coverage.  Otherwise, many subtests are skipped.
Checking OSTCOUNT and skipping the whole test would be even worse.

Many subtests could run on fewer OSTs, but that is for another patch.

Hopefully this will work for Janitor, since it only runs with
OSTCOUNT=2 today.  Autotest already runs with OSTCOUNT=8.
