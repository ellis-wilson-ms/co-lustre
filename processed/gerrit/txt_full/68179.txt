--- Commit Message ---
LU-20087 tests: fix sanity-sec/27ad hang with shared key

test_27ad puts the NID of an already mounted client into a newly
created nodemap. With shared secret key the nodemap of an export
is bound at authentication, so the servers keep the client in its
former nodemap and reject the change:

  nodemap_add_member() lustre-OST0000: error adding to nodemap
  test27ad_nm, inconsistent with nodemap default used in
  authentication: rc = -1

The rejection is returned on every connect attempt, so once the
client reconnects the import stays in DISCONN and the setattr that
touch issues waits until the session times out.
