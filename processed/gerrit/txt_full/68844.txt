LU-8622 tests: keep recovery-small 24b pages dirty

test_24b writes to two files, has the OST evict the client
and expects the eviction to discard the dirty pages, which
prints the "dirty page discard:" warning it greps for. Both
files can lose their dirty pages before the eviction lands,
and the test then fails with "no discarded dirty page
found!". Janitor hits this on ZFS only, 163 times in 30
days.

The write that failed in test_24a leaves ar_force_sync set
on the whole OSC, so the first write here is done
synchronously and is never cached. The second file is
cached, but writeback is free to flush it during the second
or so it takes the eviction to reach the client.

Spend one successful write to clear the forced sync i/o
carried over from test_24a, and stall OSC writeback for a
fixed ten seconds, which outlasts the eviction. Convert the
bare lfs and lctl calls in this test to $LFS and $LCTL while
here.

Fixes: 624a3ac23393 ("LU-921 llite: warning in case of discarding dirty pages")
