--- Commit Message ---
LU-20681 llite: guard d_add() against a hashed dentry

d_add() rehashes its dentry unconditionally, and ll_splice_alias() is
also called with hashed dentries.  Such a dentry gets relinked into
the bucket it already sits on and its d_hash.next ends up pointing at
itself, so a later __d_lookup() on that bucket spins forever.

Unhash the dentry before d_add().

Fixes: 7334a2d33cc4 ("LU-8371 llite: optimize atomic_open of negative dentry.")
