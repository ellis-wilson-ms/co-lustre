--- Commit Message ---
LU-20264 llite: plumb mnt_idmap through xattr operations

Add a struct mnt_idmap parameter to the md_setxattr()/md_getxattr()
md_ops methods and pass the VFS-provided idmap down from the llite
xattr, acl and layout paths through lmv to mdc, where it is used to
map the setxattr fsuid/fsgid and tag the request with rq_idmap.
Internal callers (crypto contexts, layout fetch, link xattr) pass
NULL, i.e. the identity mapping.

Test-Parameters: trivial
