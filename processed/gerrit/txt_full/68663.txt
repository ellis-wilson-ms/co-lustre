LU-19310 lnet: do not assert a router credit implies a buffer

lnet_post_routed_recv_locked() takes a router buffer credit before it
knows a buffer is available: when rbp_credits goes negative the msg is
parked on rbp_msgs with msg_rtrcredit set and msg_kiov still NULL.

Disabling routing frees the router buffer pools, and
lnet_rtrpool_free_bufs() cancels everything left on rbp_msgs, so such a
msg reaches lnet_return_rx_credits_locked(), which asserts that a msg
holding a router credit also holds a buffer:

  LNetError: (lib-move.c:1047:lnet_return_rx_credits_locked())
  ASSERTION( msg->msg_kiov != ((void *)0) ) failed

Skip the buffer accounting for such a msg instead.  The credit is not
given back: the pool is being torn down and its counters are reset
regardless, and lnet_finalize() may defer the msg to another thread that
completes it after that reset.
