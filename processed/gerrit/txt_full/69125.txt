LU-19079 sec: guard gss key helpers with HAVE_GSS_KEYRING

"gss_rename_sk_key()" and "gss_cleanup_sk_key()" are declared
under HAVE_GSS but those are defined in gss_keyring.c which is only
built with gss-keyring enabled. A build with gss on and gss-keyring
off (--enable-gss --disable-gss-keyring) leaves lustre.ko with
unresolved symbols. This results in  modprobe failing with "Unknown
symbol gss_rename_sk_key" and mount returns ENODEV.

Fix the corresponding #ifdef.
