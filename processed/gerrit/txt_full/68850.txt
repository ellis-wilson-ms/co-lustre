--- Commit Message ---
LU-17296 tests: test mClock fair share with no rule

The mClock policy with the weight dimension and no rule shares the
service between jobs with no configuration. Each class bucket takes
the weight of the default rule. The active buckets then get equal
shares.

Add sanityn/test_77x{a,b,c,d}. 77xa compares FIFO and the weight share
under an unequal fio pair. 77xb and 77xc show that a weight rule, or a
wildcard job ID rule, changes the share. 77xd starts the policy on the
MDT.

Document the mode in nrs_policies.4.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanityn env=ONLY="77xa 77xb 77xc 77xd",ONLY_REPEAT=3
