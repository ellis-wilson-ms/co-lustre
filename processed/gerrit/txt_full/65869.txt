LU-20257 tests: no check_and_cleanup_lustre in test

The check_and_cleanup_lustre() function is intended only for use
at the end of a test session, since it triggers logging and other
session-specific operations.  It should not be used in the middle
of a test to do unmount/cleanup of the targets and modules.
