LU-20426 lnet: reset LNet statistics over Netlink

"lnetctl stats show" and "lnetctl stats reset" are the last LNet
statistics paths still carried over ioctl, while net, peer and route
already moved to the Netlink generic interface. Their fixed ioctl
structures cannot grow a field without breaking the ABI, which leaves
no room to add statistics controls.

Add a doit to LNET_CMD_STATS that resets the counters, and drive both
show and reset from lnetctl over Netlink. lnetctl issues the Netlink
request first and falls back to the existing ioctl when the running
module does not provide the command, so a newer lnetctl still drives
an older module.

The op gains GENL_ADMIN_PERM, which genl checks before it splits doit
from dumpit. "lnetctl stats show" therefore now needs CAP_NET_ADMIN,
matching the net, peer and route ops and the privilege the ioctl path
already required.

The Netlink reply is rendered at the LNet default indent of six
spaces, while the ioctl path renders through cYAML at four. The keys
and their order do not change, but "lnetctl stats show" output that is
scraped by column position needs an update.
