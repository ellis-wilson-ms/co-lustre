--- Commit Message ---
LU-15909 lnet: use 64-bit selection sequence numbers

LNet breaks ties between nets, NIs and peer NIs by round robin. Each
selection copies a send counter into the chosen object. The object
with the lowest value wins the next tie.

The counters are 32 bits. After a wrap the newest object holds the
lowest value. It then wins every tie until the counter again passes
the values that the other objects kept. Round robin stops for a full
wrap period.

Make ln_net_seq, lp_send_seq, net_seq, ni_seq, lpn_seq and lpni_seq
u64. They then do not wrap in practice.
