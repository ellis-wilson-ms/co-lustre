LU-16678 obdclass: limit QoS weight by bytes per inode

The QoS weight of an OST is only its free space, so an OST holding
many small objects gets new objects until it runs out of inodes, while
the weight of an MDT multiplies its free space and inodes, so free
space the MDT can't use for lack of inodes still raises its weight.

Add lu_tgt_qos_avail() to count the free space and free inodes of a
target only as far as the other can use them, with a factor of 2
slack each way, at the free space per inode of the empty target.
Only ldiskfs targets have a fixed number of inodes to limit this way.
Use it for the weights in lu_tgt_qos_weight_calc(), and for the
penalties and the choice between round-robin and QoS allocation in
ltd_qos_penalties_calc() and lod_pool_qos_penalties_calc().  An OST
formatted with 1 MiB per inode, with 1 TiB and 100K inodes free, then
counts as 200 GiB free.  The bytes per inode used so far would reflect
the objects better, but precreated empty objects skew it enough on a
new OST to invert the weights.  Also recalculate the weights when only
the free inodes of a target change.

Add sanity test_51k to check that an OST with most of its inodes used
by empty objects gets a lower QoS weight.
