LU-20564 utils: probe md RAID geometry for OST mkfs/mount

mkfs.lustre now probes the md array under an OST target, through any
stacked dm devices, and seeds -E stride/stripe_width so mballoc can
align allocations to full stripes.  mke2fs can derive these from the
blkid topology, but dm-thin and dm-cache replace optimal_io_size with
their block size when that does not divide the row, and dm-vdo always
reports 4KiB.  raid0, raid4/5/6, raid10 and raidkm (level 71) are
decoded, and user-supplied values are respected.  mount.lustre warns
when an OST on a striped array has no fs geometry, or geometry that
is not a multiple of the array row, unless it is mounted with
-o stripe=.

mkfs.lustre also warns about two things that cannot be corrected once
the OST is formatted: a partition that does not start on a stripe
boundary, and, on parity arrays, a data row (k * chunk) that is not
a power of two.  The default bulk RPC sizes are powers of two, so a
row that is not one leaves a partial row at the tail of every RPC,
which the array services as a read-modify-write.  mkfs.lustre.8
gains a RAID ALIGNMENT section covering both, and how to correct
them.

Tested on RHEL 9.7 against live loop-backed md arrays: raid0/4/5/6,
raid10 near/far/offset, a non-power-of-two row, user-supplied values,
dm-linear over one array and spanning two, and misaligned partition
starts.  Also tested against live raidkm arrays, plain and
declustered.  In every case the computed row matches md's own
queue/optimal_io_size.
