**[Marc Bot - AI review - opus]**

Needs a refresh: several field descriptions and all three examples contradict what liblustreapi actually does. I checked the examples by running them against the library on a local tree; details are in the (defect) comments.

While it's being refreshed: about a third of the page (Internal Traversal Implementation, Work Queue Architecture, Queue Operations, and the path-buffer and fd notes) documents private internals that callers can't use, such as llapi_semantic_traverse(), work_unit_create_and_add() and the fwq_* fields. That text has already drifted from the code. On error the worker doesn't broadcast; parallel_find() does that after draining the queue. And the queue is a single shared FIFO, not work-stealing. Consider replacing it with the contract callers can rely on: with fp_thread_count > 1, callbacks run concurrently on up to that many threads. Each queued directory gets its own copy of find_param, so a callback's writes to param fields don't carry across directories, while the fp_cb_data pointer is shared. Visit order is not depth-first.
