LU-20932 lnet: make route round robin wrap-safe

The route round robin counter lr_seq is a signed int. After
2^31 routed sends to one remote net, the counter wraps.

LU-12756 changed the best route compare in
lnet_find_route_locked() to a plain compare. After a wrap, one
route takes all the traffic for about 2^31 sends. Then the
cycle starts again. LU-18444 also changed the last_route compare
to a plain compare. Since then, one route takes all the traffic
after the wrap, and the round robin does not recover.

Make lr_seq unsigned. Compare the counters with a signed
difference, as time_after() does.

Since LU-18444, router_sensitivity_percentage has no effect.
Update the module parameter comment, the lnetctl help text
and the liblnetconfig.h comment to say so. Also correct a
comment in lnet_health_check(). Route aliveness uses only
the NI status. Peer NI health ranks gateways and MR
forwarding paths.
