LU-10026 ofd: fix punch vs BRW deadlock on compressed files

ofd_object_punch_compr() converts a compressed chunk back into a
plain one when a truncate lands in the middle of it.  It asks the
OSD for the chunk pages first and takes the chunk range lock
afterwards, while ofd_preprw_read_compr() and
ofd_preprw_write_compr() take the range lock first and only then
ask the OSD for the pages.

A truncate and a BRW touching the same chunk deadlock on that: the
punch thread holds the page locks that osd_bufs_get() acquired and
sleeps in range_lock(), while the BRW thread holds the chunk range
lock and sleeps in osd_get_page() waiting for one of those pages.
Nothing breaks the cycle, so the OST_PUNCH never completes and the
client stays blocked in ftruncate().  sanity-compr test_fsx trips
this a minute or two after fsx starts and then eats the whole
session timeout.

Take the chunk range lock before mapping the chunks to OSD buffers
so that all three paths lock the range before the pages.
