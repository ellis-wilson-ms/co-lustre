LU-19779 ofd: log FIDs on ENOSPC in ofd_commitrw_write()

Log the OST object FID, its parent FID and its stripe index when a
write hits ENOSPC in ofd_commitrw_write().  The first three ENOSPC
results are logged at D_INODE while the write is retried after a
forced commit, and the one that gives up reaches the console at
D_WARNING.  This lets an administrator identify the files impacted
by a near-full OST and run OSTs closer to full capacity.

filter_fid::ff_parent::f_ver holds the index of the object in the
parent layout, so the parent FID is printed with f_ver cleared and
the stripe index printed on its own, keeping the FID usable with
"lfs fid2path" as printed.
