LU-12550 ldlm: extent expansion with new_contention signal

Introduce ldlm_resource_is_contended() in ldlm extent
expansion fixup, which checks whether the resource is still
in the contention hold window.

When contended, reset expand start to the requested start.
Cap expanded end to req_start + LDLM_MAX_GROWN_EXTENT for
small requests; cap to req_end + LDLM_MAX_GROWN_EXTENT
otherwise.

This helps to reduce lock cancellation.
