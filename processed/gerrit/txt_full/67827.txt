LU-20264 ptlrpc: carry a mnt_idmap in ptlrpc_request

Add rq_idmap to ptlrpc_request and map current's fsuid/fsgid
through it in the generic body, jobinfo and user-desc packers via
lustre_current_fsuid/fsgid(). This also switches the uid/gid slots
of these wire structures from current's real uid/gid to fsuid/
fsgid, which is what the servers should be checking against.

No caller sets rq_idmap yet, so the NULL idmap keeps the identity
mapping and behavior is unchanged.

Test-Parameters: trivial
