LU-20655 efalnd: Fix wrong refcounting on launch Tx errors

On several flows like GETR ACK or FRWR completion we call launch Tx to
continue to the next step stage of the Tx work. Those flows expect that
a refcount will be taken on the Tx in launch Tx before returning to the
caller or else it can potentially call Tx done function on a Tx that
isn't completed yet. Launch Tx currently takes the refcount only when
the Tx was successfully posted to the device but this can fail if the QP
is full or the connection isn't active.

This can lead to undefined behaviors on both sides of the transaction:
- Initiator can potentially crash because of a race created between one
  thread posting all pending TXs (including the one failed before) and
  the thread completing the Tx.
- Receiver can receive the done message without receiving the payload at
  all.

To fix this, we take the Tx refcount once the Tx is placed on the
connection pending list. This means the Tx is committed to be sent
eventually (it can still be canceled). This eliminates the race since
now every Tx being sent by launch Tx has at least a single refcount.
