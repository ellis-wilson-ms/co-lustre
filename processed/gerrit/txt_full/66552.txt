LU-1158 general: rename timestamp fields to _ns

Now that the in-memory timestamp fields hold epoch nanoseconds,
rename them from xxx_(a/m/c)time to xxx_(a/m/c)time_ns so the
field name reflects the unit.  This covers the struct lu_attr and
cl_attr fields, the cached llite inode times, md_op_data
op_mod_time, and the nanosecond interval tunables (ofd_atime_diff,
OFD_DEF_ATIME_DIFF, ofo_atime_ondisk, mdd_atime_diff,
MAX_ATIME_DIFF and the ldlm ns_ctime_age_limit).

The wire fields of struct obdo, mdt_body, ost_lvb and mdt_rec_*
keep their names, since their unit depends on whether the peer
negotiated OBD_CONNECT_NANOSEC_TIME; document that at the flag.

This patch is a pure rename with no functional change.
