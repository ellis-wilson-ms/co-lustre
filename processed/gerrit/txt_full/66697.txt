LU-14291 ptlprc: use CONFIG_LUSTRE_FS_SERVER

We see the improper use of HAVE_SERVER_SUPPORT in the kernel code.
HAVE_SERVER_SUPPORT is only for the user land code. Convert to 
CONFIG_LUSTRE_FS_SERVER and update spelling.txt to
avoid this issue again, hopefully.
