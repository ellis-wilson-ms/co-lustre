LU-16518 ptlrpc: remove unused helper functions

... since they are no longer used. The last callers of
thread_is_init(), thread_set_flags() and lustre_shrink_reply()
were removed a long time ago. binheap_size(),
binheap_remove_root(), nrs_pol2svcpt() and
lustre_swab_batch_update_request() have never been used since
they were introduced, and ptlrpc_restart_req(),
lustre_request_magic() and lustre_swab_lmv_desc() have been
unused for as long as the tree has git history.
