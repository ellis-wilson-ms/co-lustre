--- Commit Message ---
LU-20204 obdclass: adjust object hash size

Having 100m locks on server gives an avg length of a handles hash
queue ~1500. Having many CANCEL RPCs with ~500 locks each, makes
all ldlm threads busy with just searching for locks in the hash.

In fact, the hash size is not approprate for that amount of locks
on server. At the same time, we know in advance the amount of locks
will be stored in hash as we have a limit for ldlm pools - we try to
balance the pool sizes about that limit. Choose an approprate hash
size at the setup.
