--- Commit Message ---
LU-20584 target: init nfd_skip_ipv6 in tgt_import_update()

tgt_import_update() leaves nfd_skip_ipv6 and nfd_has_ipv6
uninitialized on the stack. server_nid2radix() reads
nfd_skip_ipv6 and drops every large NID when it is set, so
the NID fetch can return nothing and the target reports
"can't get local NIDs from LNet, rc = -100".

The MGS then never learns the NIDs of a target that did not
restart with it, and a client reachable only over one of
those NIDs cannot connect to that target.

This notification always carries large NIDs, so clear both
flags.
