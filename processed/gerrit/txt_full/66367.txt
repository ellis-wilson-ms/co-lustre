LU-20191 efalnd: Split conn cleanup into scan and drain stages

Split kefalnd_cleanup_ni_conns into two stages to reduce write lock
hold time and improve cleanup responsiveness:

Stage 1 (kefalnd_scan_ni_conns, every 5s): Scan the hash table for
idle or deactivating connections. Under read lock collect candidates
onto a per-NI cleanup list. Under write lock re-validate and remove
confirmed idle connections from the hash table.

Stage 2 (kefalnd_drain_ni_conns, every 1s): Iterate the per-NI
cleanup list without holding conn_lock. Drain abort and pending TXs
and destroy fully drained connections. Early return when the list is
empty so there is no cost in the common case.

This eliminates the fixed MAX_IDLE_CONN cap, moves TX cleanup out of
the write lock critical section, uses a consistent timestamp for TX
timeout checks, and ensures connections pending cleanup are also
destroyed on NI shutdown.
