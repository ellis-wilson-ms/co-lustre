LU-20344 lnet: scope LNetAddPeer primary to reachable NIDs

LNetAddPeer() adds each NID from the config log, also on nets that
this node cannot reach. With lock_prim_nid set, it locks the first
NID as the primary. Discovery cannot delete or change a locked
primary.

In REACHABLE mode, LNetAddPeer() now skips the unreachable NIDs, and
locks the first reachable NID. When no NID is reachable, it adds no
peer. Traffic can then make an unlocked peer before the next config
log event. When a locked peer already holds a listed NID,
LNetAddPeer() merges the other NIDs into that peer. A net that comes
up between two calls then cannot make two locked peers.
