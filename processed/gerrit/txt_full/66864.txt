--- Commit Message ---
LU-20426 lnet: reset per-NI and per-peer-NI stats

"lnetctl stats reset" clears only the global LNet counters. The send,
receive and drop counts and the per-operation latency accumulators
kept on each local NI and peer NI are left untouched, so the only way
to clear them is to tear down the interface or peer, or reload the
module.

Have the reset walk every local NI and peer NI and zero their element
stats and latency accumulators alongside the global counters, under
the same IOC_LIBCFS_RESET_LNET_STATS path. The walk holds the
exclusive net lock; concurrent data-path updates race benignly, as the
values are telemetry.
