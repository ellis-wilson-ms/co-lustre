--- Commit Message ---
LU-20099 tests: add sanityn/test_77vj for mClock R|L|W

Add sanityn/test_77vj. The test runs mClock with the reserve, limit
and weight dimensions in 4 scenarios. Two fio flows read two files.
Each flow runs 8 jobs at an I/O depth of 16. Each flow matches one
rule, with weight 100 or 200.

Scenario 1 measures the total. Scenario 2 checks both reservations.
Flow 1 reserves more than its weight share, so the check fails when
the scheduler ignores the reservation. Scenario 3 limits both flows
and checks both ceilings. Scenario 4 limits flow 2 only. It checks
that ceiling, and it checks that flow 1 gets more than its
reservation.

Each ceiling is 10 or 20 percent of the total. A scheduler that
ignores the limit gives a flow 2.5 to 3 times the 110 percent bar. A
total below 20 IOPS skips scenarios 2 to 4.
