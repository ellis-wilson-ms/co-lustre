LU-20211 idl: reserve OBD_CONNECT2_LAYOUT_REPLACE flag

Reserve OBD_CONNECT2_LAYOUT_REPLACE at 0x8000000000000ULL for an MDS
that replaces the layout of a file on trunc(0) when some stripes are
inactive.  Only the flag, obd_connect_names[] and the wire checks are
added here so the value can land before the feature patch.
