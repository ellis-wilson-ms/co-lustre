--- Commit Message ---
LU-20251 osd-wbcfs: fix dentry pinning

Swapping kill_litter_super() to kill_anon_super() removes the
implicit d_genocide() that cleans up stale dentry during umount.
This causes warnings and hangs during umount. Refactor osd-wbcfs
to handle these changes better.
