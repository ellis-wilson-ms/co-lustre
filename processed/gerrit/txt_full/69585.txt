LU-16932 tests: observe HSM requests before shortening timeout

A one-second request timeout can expire before the test sees
STARTED. This fails test_251 and leaves the shortened timeout
in effect for later tests, including test_254b.

Shorten the timeout after observing STARTED and restore the
coordinator parameters on every exit from the test.
