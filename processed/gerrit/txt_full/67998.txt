--- Commit Message ---
LU-20566 ec: reuse surviving DIO bounce pages on EC recovery

When llite.*.ec_dio_reuse is enabled, preserve successfully filled
unaligned DIO bounce pages across the CIT_EC_RD restart and adopt
them as EC_DPG_UPTODATE so healthy stripes are not re-fetched.
Layout generation is checked after locks are held; on mismatch the
container is dropped and full recovery proceeds. Default off.
Encrypted files, aligned DIO, and AIO are excluded from reuse.
