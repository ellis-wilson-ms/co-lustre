LU-20714 lov: bound foreign LOV EA reads by buffer size

The foreign layout EA reaches the client as a reply buffer of
mbo_eadatasize bytes, and its readers trust it. ll_manage_foreign()
checks only that the length is non-zero before reading lfm_magic and
lfm_type, and lsme_unpack_foreign() reads both before it bounds
lfm_length, so a buffer shorter than the 16-byte lov_foreign_md
header is read past its end. lsm_unpackmd_foreign() never compares
lfm_length with buf_size at all, then memcpy()s that many bytes out,
and readlink() or lfs getstripe hands the result to userspace.

Bound all three reads by the buffer the EA arrived in.
lod_verify_striping() bounds a foreign layout when it is set, so
reaching any of them needs a corrupt on-disk EA or a hostile MDS.
