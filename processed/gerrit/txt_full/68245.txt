--- Commit Message ---
LU-17000 ldlm: report allocation failures from ldlm_setup()

GOTO(label, rc) does not assign its second argument, so the three
bare GOTO(out, -ENOMEM) sites left rc at 0.  ldlm_setup() then ran
ldlm_cleanup(), which frees ldlm_state, and still reported success,
leaving ldlm_refcount at 1 with a NULL ldlm_state for the next
caller to dereference.
