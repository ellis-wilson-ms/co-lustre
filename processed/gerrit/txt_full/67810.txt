LU-20354 quota: parallelize usage-cache GETOQUOTA

Refresh stale per-slave usage in parallel batches via quf_fetch_batch.
MDT queues remote OST Q_GETOQUOTA on a ptlrpc_set through LOD/OSP
o_quotactl_async. Local MDT (DOM) stays a synchronous lquotactl_slv
in the same batch; remote MDT remains -EOPNOTSUPP.

On fetch failure keep the pre-fetch snap_usage when the slot was
previously populated, otherwise fall back to granted. Re-lookup the
slot by key after the unlocked fetch; shrink waits on lqu_fetching
so the entry is still present. This cuts cold-miss / TTL-expire
latency on large OST counts without changing GETQUOTA fallback
semantics for OST slaves.
