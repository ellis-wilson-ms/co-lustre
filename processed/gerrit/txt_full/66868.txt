LU-20427 tests: Incorrect debug mask in sanity-lnet

Patch https://review.whamcloud.com/66451 dropped "neterror net
nettrace malloc" arguments from set_default_debug and moved them to
SUBSYSTEM environment variable at the top of sanity-lnet.sh, but
SUBSYSTEM is the variable used to set subsystem_debug mask, not the
debug mask. The correct variable is PTLDEBUG.
