--- Commit Message ---
LU-10026 sec: set page sync flags to avoid ENOMEM

Set OBD_BRW_SOFT_SYNC page flag when appropriate page_pool
is at least 75% full. This would cause a "soft sync" update
at OST side. When pool is at least 81% full clear OBD_BRW_ASYNC
flag to trigger a sync commit. This needs to not stop
compression at client side when page pool becomes empty,
i.e. no free elements.

There is sanity-compr_2001 to check that all chunks from written
1GB data have been successfully compressed, but it will be
landed later,
in a separate patch, after the statistics patches.
