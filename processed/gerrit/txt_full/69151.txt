--- Commit Message ---
LU-20814 utils: keep errno across errlog() in l_getsepol

Callers of errlog() return -errno right after it. But errlog()
calls isatty() and the syslog functions, which can change errno.
In CI, under call_usermodehelper(), isatty() left ENOTTY, so the
real error (-13) was reported as -25. Save errno on entry and
restore it on exit.

errlog() also passes the same va_list to vsyslog() and then to
vfprintf(), which is undefined. Start it again for vfprintf().
