LU-18601 tests: keep sanity 205b jobid_var on the client

test_205b read the old jobid_var from mds1 but restored it on the
client. After test_205a the MDS can still have jobid_var=disable,
and Maloo logs of the 205l failures show 205b then setting
jobid_var=disable on the client. test_205l set jobid_var only on
the MDTs, but the jobid is made on the client, so it got no jobs
and failed with "0 jobs".

Save the client value in 205b, and set jobid_var on the client in
205l. 205l already saves and restores the client value.
