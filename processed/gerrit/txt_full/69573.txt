--- Commit Message ---
LU-19932 llite: Treat direct write as sync write again

LU-16695 dropped O_DIRECT from wr_sync when switching to ki_flags.
When a DIO write falls back to a buffered write (e.g. unaligned DIO to
OSTs without unaligned DIO support, or -EBUSY from invalidating cached
pages) and pages are committed to OSC once the buffered write reaches
64MiB (PTLRPC_MAX_BRW_SIZE), the last extent committed by the write
cl_io stays active until its cl_io_end. Before that, the same thread
runs direct_write_fallback(), whose nested fsync cl_io waits for that
extent, so the thread deadlocks with itself.

Include IOCB_DIRECT in wr_sync again so the extent is released at
commit in cl_io_start instead of at cl_io_end.
