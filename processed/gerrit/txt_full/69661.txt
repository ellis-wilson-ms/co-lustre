--- Commit Message ---
LU-20948 o2iblnd: renew a FastReg MR after a failed tx

o2iblnd posts LOCAL_INV and REG_MR before a FastReg RDMA. It bumps
the MR key when it maps the descriptor. A QP flush can drop both work
requests, so the HCA keeps the old key. The next LOCAL_INV on that
descriptor then fails, and the QP goes to error. Each conn that gets
the descriptor fails the same way, until o2iblnd frees the idle pool.

A tx can fail after o2iblnd posts its LOCAL_INV and REG_MR. Then
kiblnd_fmr_pool_unmap() gives the descriptor a new MR, or frees the
descriptor if the allocation fails. A simulated send error posts
nothing, so the descriptor keeps its LOCAL_INV for the next post.

    Soft-RoCE, 2 VMs, lst brw 1M read and write, 5 s RoCE drop
    during load, 6 cycles
    without the fix: bulk 0 MiB/s after cycle 1, 43 LOCAL_INV
                     failures in rxe_invalidate_mr()
    with the fix:    bulk 14-30 MiB/s in each cycle, no LOCAL_INV
                     failures, 29 MR renewals
