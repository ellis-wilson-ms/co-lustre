--- Commit Message ---
LU-20264 llite: pass the VFS idmap to create operations

Pass the VFS-provided struct mnt_idmap from ll_mknod(),
ll_symlink() and ll_mkdir() down to ll_new_node() and the intent
mkdir path, store it in op_idmap and re-resolve the new inode's
owner with it. The VFS map may be more accurate than the mount
idmap that ll_prep_md_op_data() used.

Test-Parameters: trivial
