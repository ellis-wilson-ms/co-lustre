--- Commit Message ---
LU-20315 lnet: mark selftest init functions __init

srpc_startup(), sfw_startup(), lstcon_console_init() and the helpers
they call for the RPC module, acceptor service and netlink family are
only reachable from lnet_selftest_init(), which is already __init.

Mark them __init too, along with the BUILD_BUG_ON wrapper in
module.c, so the kernel frees them once module load completes
instead of keeping them resident for the life of the module.
