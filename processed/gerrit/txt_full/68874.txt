--- Commit Message ---
LU-20661 ptlrpc: Validate the PM QoS tunables

pmqos_latency_max_usec accepted negative values, which are not a
valid latency constraint and leave cpuidle nothing but polling;
pmqos_default_duration_usec accepted values that usecs_to_jiffies()
turns into 0, taking and dropping the constraint on every RPC.
Reject both at the sysfs store rather than clamping them downstream.

Add the newline the two debug messages next to them were missing.

Fixes: 54a64ea818d ("LU-18446 ptlrpc: lower CPUs latency during client I/O")
