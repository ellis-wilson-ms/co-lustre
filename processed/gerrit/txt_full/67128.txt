LU-19848 lnet: add p2pdma counters

Add a new set of counters to track P2P DMA (GPU device memory)
send/recv traffic. A message is flagged as P2P DMA when its MD
carries LNET_MD_FLAG_P2PDMA; the flag is cached on the lnet_msg
at MD attach time, since the MD is detached before the message
is decommitted. The per-CPT counters are bumped at decommit, and
only for the events that actually move the MD payload, matching
how lcc_send_length/lcc_recv_length are accounted:

  send: PUT sent (LNET_EVENT_SEND), GET served (LNET_EVENT_GET)
  recv: PUT received (LNET_EVENT_PUT), REPLY (LNET_EVENT_REPLY)

ACKs and the GET request itself are not counted, and the flag is
cleared before the message is reused to send an ACK.

The aggregated counts are exposed via the two trailing fields of
/sys/kernel/debug/lnet/stats, and as p2pdma_send_count and
p2pdma_recv_count in "lnetctl stats show" (both the netlink and
the ioctl paths).

The counters are appended to struct lnet_counters, which grows
struct lnet_ioctl_lnet_stats. IOC_LIBCFS_GET_LNET_STATS now
accepts the shorter buffer passed by older tools and only fills
in what fits, so an old lnetctl keeps working with a new module.

Update sanity test_215 to accept the two new trailing fields in
lnet.stats, while still accepting the old 11 field format.
