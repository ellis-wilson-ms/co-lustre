--- Commit Message ---
LU-17408 mdt: fix barrier call order in mdt_quotactl()

mdt_quotactl() calls barrier_exit() before barrier_entry(), so a
Q_SETQUOTA or Q_SETINFO rejected by a frozen barrier leaves bi_writers
at -1 and no later barrier_freeze() completes until the MDT is
remounted.  Enter the barrier before the quota update and leave it on
every exit path, and set rc when the barrier rejects the request, which
was silently returning success.  Add sanity test_801d.
