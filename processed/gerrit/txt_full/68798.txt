--- Commit Message ---
LU-10026 osc: do not expand if overflow compression

The extent expansion function osc_extent_expand() can
expand extents beyond the compression chunk limit, completely
bypassing the limit enforced in osc_extent_find() and
try_to_add_extent_for_io().

Let's add the check to the osc_extent_expand() to
make this situation impossible.
