LU-18864 lst: resolve supplied NIDs to peer primary

lst keys a test node on the NID that the operator gives. A secondary
NID of a multi-rail peer makes a second node for that peer. LNet on
the console matches each reply on the peer primary NID. The reply then
misses the request, and lst fails:

    create session RPC failed on <id>: Unknown error -110

Resolve each supplied NID with LNetPrimaryNID() before the node hash
lookup. A NID that LNet cannot resolve keeps its value. The test RPCs
carry only 4-byte NIDs, so a large primary NID does not replace the
supplied NID. With discovery disabled, the NID becomes the primary
NID that LNet holds.

LNetPrimaryNID() can wait for discovery under ses_mutex. Stop the
NID list on a signal. Renew ses_laststamp after each NID, so that
the pinger does not expire the session.
