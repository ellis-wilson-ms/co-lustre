--- Commit Message ---
LU-20264 llite: set op_idmap in lookup, open and unlink paths

Pass the caller's mnt_idmap into ll_lookup_it() and
ll_intent_file_open() and store it in op_idmap so intent lookups
and opens carry the right mapping; atomic open and file open use
file_mnt_idmap() while plain lookup falls back to the mount idmap.
Set op_idmap explicitly for rmdir and unlink as well.
