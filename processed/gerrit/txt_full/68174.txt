--- Commit Message ---
LU-16518 sec: remove unused FS_CTX_REQUIRES_FREE_ENCRYPT_FL

This flag was only ever stored in the "flags" field of struct
llcrypt_ctx: llcrypt_get_ctx() set it and llcrypt_release_ctx()
tested it to decide whether to free the context or return it to the
preallocated pool. Commit 1c91e657e35c ("LU-16518 sec: remove unused
llcrypt compat functions") removed both functions and struct
llcrypt_ctx itself, leaving the definition without a user.

Fixes: 1c91e657e35c ("LU-16518 sec: remove unused llcrypt compat functions")
