LU-20178 osc: wire protocol and UAPI definitions for CSDC

Add wire protocol structures and UAPI definitions for
client-side data compression (CSDC). This includes:

- New compression type and chunk size fields in struct
  lov_comp_md_entry_v1 for per-component compression
  settings
- OBD_CONNECT2_COMPRESS connect flag for client/server
  negotiation of compression capability
- UAPI header lustre_compr.h defining supported
  compression algorithms (lz4fast, lz4hc, lzo), compression
  levels, and chunk sizes (64KB to 4MB)
- Wire protocol test updates in wirecheck.c and
  wiretest.c to validate the new fields
