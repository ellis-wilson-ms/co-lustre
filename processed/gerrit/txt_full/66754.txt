LU-20376 ptlrpc: use rq_no_delay for final ping

recovery-small test_170 shows one more problem during recovery.
If reconnection happens during REPLAY_WAIT and final ping is
not sent, it stays inflight without ability to be sent after
reconnection, use rq_no_delay for it. The
ptlrpc_import_recovery_state_machine() will send it after
reconnection.

test_170 is improved with a limit waiting, and skipping fast
REPLAY_LOCK phase.
