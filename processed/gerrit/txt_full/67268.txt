LU-20482 lnet: fix BAD_CONFIG hang on secondary NID change

When a multi-rail peer's secondary NID is renumbered while its
primary remains reachable, LNetAddPeer() builds a temporary peer
for the new NID with lp_merge_primary_nid set, expecting
discovery to merge it into the existing peer lp2. The merge in
lnet_peer_add_nid() trips the LOCK_PRIMARY && lp_nnis > 1 safety
check, marks both peers BAD_CONFIG, and hangs all I/O to the
server.

Allow the merge to proceed when lp_merge_primary_nid is set,
i.e. when the caller explicitly requested this merge via
LNetAddPeer(). The original guard is preserved for all other
callers.
